Cooking mode, S3b alarm with "Stop and go to step" (web)

Plan 0111 (S3b), R16. The alarm opened OUTSIDE that recipe's cooking mode adds one quiet text action under "+ 10 min": "Stop and go to step N" (cooking.alarm.goToStep). It does what Stop alarm does, then opens that recipe's cooking mode at step N, pushed, so Back returns to where the cook was; focus lands on the step's focus line. Stop alarm keeps the one fill and first focus; tab order Stop alarm, + 5 min, + 10 min, Stop and go to step. The dialog is labelled by the headline and described by the step text (native: the container accessibility label). Escape is Stop alarm. Hidden when the recipe is known to be gone. Inside the recipe's own cooking mode the action is not shown (the S17 board).
