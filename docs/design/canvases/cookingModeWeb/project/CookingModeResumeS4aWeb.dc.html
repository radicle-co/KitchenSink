Cooking mode, S4a leave and come back (web)

S4a. Under 4 hours, Resume goes straight to the step with its lines above it, in this order, which the build must assert: 1, the welcome line (welcomeBack, welcomeBackJustNow or welcomeBackChanged); 2, recipeChanged; 3, the timer line (timerKept, timerEnded, timerEndedJustNow); 4, S31's offline notice. welcomeBackChanged (the step moved) and recipeChanged (the step stayed, the recipe changed) never show together. The step heading takes focus; the line region mounts empty and gets its text after the focus move, so it is announced once. At 4 hours or more, "Carry on from earlier?" comes first and takes focus, and the lines sit under it. "Just now" (under 1 minute) uses its own keys, never "just now ago". Dock rows are not live regions.
