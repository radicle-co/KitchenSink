How to copy a whole page

Open the page’s source, select it all, copy it, then paste it here.

  • Chrome or Edge: press Ctrl+U, or Option+Command+U on a Mac. Then press Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C, or Command+A and Command+C on a Mac.
  • Firefox: press Ctrl+U, or Command+U on a Mac. Then select all and copy.
  • Safari: first turn on the Develop menu in Safari’s Settings, under Advanced. Then press Option+Command+U, select all and copy.
