# Public version at import: SPECIFY (web and mobile)

**What this is.** The build spec for the screens of spec 003B (`specs/003B-public-rewrite/`). It covers the choice
between a private copy and a public version at the end of an import. It also covers the writing wait, the review of the
public version, and the states with only one way forward. Last come the dead end with the Pro panel, the one-time age
question and success. `fe-1`
builds from it. The web boards are drawn on `recipeImportingWeb` (§12.1). A later pass draws the mobile boards on
`recipeImportingMobile` from §4 and §12.2. It is a design document, not production code. No file it names exists until
the build makes it, unless this spec says it exists.

**Inputs.** Spec 003B: `spec.md`, `agentNotes.md`, `data-model.md` and `contracts/publicVersion.api.md`. The final copy:
v3 over v2, in `specs/003B-public-rewrite/research.md` R8, the single source. The paste-import SPECIFY document
(`docs/design/pasteImport/spec.md`): this spec extends it and follows its structure. Owner preferences:
`docs/design/uiRefreshWorkshop5/ownerFeedback.md` (N8, P4, P5, C2, E1). Also even type sizes and a 28 pt maximum. Also the
storm-ocean palette, inputs sized to content, and 320 CSS px with no horizontal scroll. Plans and prices: spec 010 `FR-041`,
`FR-041h`, `FR-042a`, `FR-042b`, `FR-042c`, `FR-042j`.

**Standing.** Written by `staff-ux-engineer`, SPECIFY mode. Every judgement about what a cook will prefer is heuristic
judgement, untested. No study of Commise users backs it.

## 1. Purpose

**User.** A home cook who imported a recipe from somewhere else, at the end of the import review. The baseline is the
cook who struggles. The cook who only wants a private copy must not be slowed down.

**Goal.** Choose, in one place, whether the recipe stays private, goes out as a public version, or both. If a public
version is chosen, read it, compare it with the cook's own copy, and publish it.

**The one thing.** The cook always knows what each choice keeps and what it shares, before pressing anything. The
choice says it in the hint of each option, and the credit preview shows the cook's name as it will appear.

On the Both path, and on any item that is already saved, every exit (Stop, Back from P7 or P8, `review.notNowBoth`, `failed.done` and the blocked and final exits) calls cancel and goes to P6, the private copy, with focus on the menu button. Nothing returns to P5. Start on an item whose `saved_at` is set answers `409 itemSaved` for either choice, and the client opens P6. On the later route (the copy is already a saved recipe) the same exits cancel only the public-version job and never touch the saved recipe. On the For everyone path Back from P7 calls no route, as the paste design does (D32 and S39 in `docs/design/pasteImport/spec.md`): the import stays in To review in My recipes, nothing is spent, and the cook sees `importMessages.leave.saved` (§7.2). editor's discard dialog), and confirming drops the unsaved edits and calls no route. On the Both path the private copy is already saved, so leaving P8 keeps it.

## 3. Layout

Container classes as in the paste spec §3 (narrow below 600 of content, regular below 960, wide from 960).

### 3.1 P5 visibility area (B1 to B4)

A `pi-block` card with an H2 and its content, between the source block and the Save area.

- **choice:** H2 `title`, then `explain` as body text, then the radio group (each row: label in bold, hint below,
  the radio aligned to the first line). For For everyone or Both, the credit preview comes next. Then `creditNote` and
  the `creditLink`. The selected row takes `selectedFill` with a `selectedEdge` outline.
- **privateOnly, publicExists, publicOnly:** H2 "Who can see it", the line, the link where the state has one, the slot
  line on Free.
- **Save area:** the paste spec's confirmation line (`importMessages.paste.review.confirmSource`, S8), then the button.

### 3.2 P7 Writing (B6)

A still `pencilLine` glyph (32), H1 `writing.title`, `writing.bodyPublic` on the For everyone path ("This takes a moment. You can leave and finish it later from To review in My recipes.") and `writing.body` on the Both path and the later route, and on the Both path and the later route a status row with a check and
`writing.bothSaved`. Stop is a secondary button (on the For everyone path it reads `review.discard`). No progress bar and no time estimate.

### 3.3 P8 Review (B7)

- **Header:** H1 `review.title`, `review.body`.
- **Name:** a text field labelled "Recipe name" (`review.titleLabel`, new) holding the built title, `review.titleNote`
  under it. The field is as wide as the column, because a title is long.
- **Steps:** H2 "Steps" (`review.stepsHeading`, new), a numbered list in a card. Under it the "Show my copy"
  disclosure. Open, it shows two lists, "Public version" and "Your copy" (`review.oursHeading` and
  `review.yourCopyHeading`, new). They sit side by side from `regular`, and stack below it.
- **Side (right column at wide, after the steps below it):** first the flags group (only with flags). Then the
  `review.photoNote` status row. Then the credit card: `credit.written`, `credit.basedOn` (free site only) and
  `review.licence`. Last, Publish (primary), Edit steps (secondary) and the not-now action (ghost).
- **Two columns at wide:** DOM order header, name, side (flags, credit, actions), steps. The grid places the steps in
  the left column, as P5 does, so Tab reaches Publish without passing every step.

### 3.4 P9 Dead end (B5)

One column, 560 wide at most: the recipe title and source line, H2 `deadEnd.title`, `deadEnd.body`, `deadEnd.free`,
then the plan card, then `notNow.note`, then the two buttons. The plan card (`paperRaised`, radius 18, the medium
shadow):

1. A head row: "Pro" (`pro.name`, 18) and, for an eligible cook only, the trial badge (`pro.trialBadge`, `selectedFill`
   with `actionText`).
2. The two prices as the most prominent figures (28, display face, never broken inside a figure), "a month" and "a
   year" under each, "or" between. They wrap as units at narrow widths. Their group's accessible name is `pro.price`.
3. `pro.saving`, computed from the store prices for the cook's country.
4. Three perks between dividers, each with a 20 px glyph in `actionText`: `lock` (private recipes), `clock` (pantry),
   `sparkle` (AI uses).
5. `pro.trial` (eligible cooks only), then `pro.renews`, then `pro.compare` as a link.

There is no "most popular", no "Recommended", no struck-through price, no countdown and no urgency word. At 1280 the
two buttons sit in one row, equal width, Upgrade first. Below 840 they stack, equal width, Upgrade at the bottom.

### 3.5 P6 Recipe detail (B9)

Unchanged layout. The credit block under the badge row, for the private copy and the public version, is owned by
`docs/design/publicVersion/detailSpec.md` §1. The cook landing here is the owner, and an owner never sees "Report a
problem" (detailSpec D2).

The public version's progress shows in that block as one status row, in the slot `done.linkPublic` takes after success (detailSpec §1.2). One shows at a time: writing, then ready, then the link. Web: one row at the full content width, the glyph and then the text, as a link-styled button that opens P7 or P8 (writing: glyph `clock`, `yourImports.publicWriting`; ready: glyph `globe`, `yourImports.publicReady`, and a trailing `chevronRight`). Mobile: the same slot, a full-width tappable row, the whole row the target, at least 48 tall (44 pt on iOS), with a trailing chevron. It is not a banner and not pinned. A change from writing to ready while the page is open is announced once in a polite live region. It shows to the owner only. After the job expires the row goes away, and "Share a public version" stays in the owner menu.

visibility area's render component and the button. The client never computes an offer. With no free private slot the server answers `deadEnd`, never `privateOnly`, for every case that would end in "Save my copy", because that save would be refused.

| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |

| `privateOnly` (`reason`)                      | One line for the reason, slot line on Free                              | "Save my copy"                                                    |

### 5.2 Per control

- **Radio rows.** Selecting changes the button label and, for For everyone and Both, shows the credit preview. It
  announces nothing beyond the radio's own state.
- **Continue (nothing chosen).** S2.
- **Choice sheet (phones).** The review page's pinned button reads `button.none` "Continue" and opens the sheet. In the
  sheet the button takes the choice's label (`button.private`, `button.public` or `button.both`) and commits. With
  nothing chosen the sheet's button also reads "Continue" and shows `pickOne` (S2). Closing the sheet (swipe down, the
  scrim, or Back) returns to the review page with nothing saved. iPad keeps the in-page card.
- **Save my copy.** Saves the private copy as the paste spec's Save does, and spends the paste's one unit. On a file item the private save is the wizard's per-recipe decision write and then advance, which cancels any job and charges nothing, because the file's unit was spent when its lines were sent. If the allowance refuses it, the screen shows `allowanceRefused.line` and nothing is saved (paste, link and share items only: a file item never meets it, FR-016a). Success is P6.
- **Write the public version / Save and write.** Starts the job (`POST …/public-version`). Both saves the private copy
  first, which spends the paste's unit; if the allowance refuses it, the screen shows `allowanceRefused.line` and no job starts. On `409 offerChanged` (body `{ code: 'offerChanged', offer }`) the visibility area re-renders from `offer`, nothing selected, and shows
  `offerChanged.line`. On `409 publicExists` it re-renders as `publicExists`. Start spends no unit for the writer job (C-24).
- **Stop.** Both: calls cancel and returns to P6, the private copy, with focus on the menu button. The copy stays. For everyone: an explicit discard. The control reads `review.discard` ("Discard this import"), calls cancel with no confirm, saves nothing and spends nothing, and the import leaves To review in My recipes. Cancel deletes the import on this path (nothing is saved, so nothing is kept). On a file item the control reads `review.discardFile` ("Discard this recipe") and calls the discard-item route, which removes that one recipe and keeps the rest of the file. On the later route Stop calls cancel and returns to P6 with focus on the menu button. Back never discards (on the For everyone path it calls no route).
- **Show my copy / Hide my copy.** A disclosure. Its state is not kept across visits.
- **Edit steps.** Turns the steps into fields. The button becomes "Done editing" (reuse the editor's key).
- **Write it again.** Calls the retry route, which starts a new attempt of the same job (up to 3). Returns to P7.
- **Publish.** `POST …/publish` with the edited title and steps. A `403 ageConfirmNeeded` answer opens P10 (X7).
  `422 flags` shows the flags group. `422 tooSimilar` shows B8 blocked. `503 checkUnavailable` shows B8 check unavailable, with Publish enabled to try again and the private exit.
  `409 privateCopyChanged` (Both) shows the flags group's rewrite action with `flag.fix` (no new string).
- **Keep it just for me / `Don’t publish` / Cancel import.** For everyone, with a free slot: saves the private copy through keepPrivate, ends the job and spends the paste’s one unit. On a file item, while the job can be kept, it calls keepPrivate, which answers `202 { importId, ordinal }`, and the client then calls advance (nothing is charged). At the attempts-used Final screen it is the wizard's per-recipe decision write and then advance. With no free slot the screen never offers it, and shows the no-slot set (§6.7). The `403 PRIVATE_SLOTS_FULL` answer can only happen in a race, when the last slot fills after the screen rendered. The screen then swaps to the no-slot set in place. Both: Stop, Back, `review.notNowBoth`, `failed.done` and the blocked and final exits call cancel, the job ends, the copy stays and the cook lands on P6.
- **Discard.** `review.discard`, paste, link and share items on the public path only, and the wording of Stop there. On a file item it is `review.discardFile`, which calls the discard-item route, removes that one recipe and announces `discard.doneFile` (or `discard.doneFileLast` for the file's last recipe). It is a secondary control, never primary. Calls cancel with no confirm (or, with no job yet, the existing import delete, `DELETE /api/v1/import/{importId}`), and, on a paste, link or share item, cancel deletes the import: nothing is saved and nothing is spent. My recipes then announces `discard.done` ("Import discarded.") as a polite status snackbar, on both platforms (WCAG 4.1.3). A file item's discard deletes no import row and spends nothing, because the file's unit is already spent. Always an explicit action, never the label of a private exit. The 24-hour expiry ends the job but not the import: it stays in To review in My recipes as an ordinary unfinished import (§6.11).
- **Allowance refused at save.** When the allowance refuses the unit as any save ends the paste (Save my copy, Save and write, keepPrivate or Publish), nothing is saved, the screen shows `allowanceRefused.line`, and the cook keeps the review as it was and can discard.
- **Upgrade to Pro.** Opens the payment page (web modal, mobile store sheet). On success the cook returns to P5 with the offer
  re-read, and hears `pro.welcome`. On cancel or failure, back to P9 unchanged.
- **Not now / Close.** Ends the import, nothing saved, announces `deadEnd.closed`, and returns to My recipes.

A row in To review in My recipes has one secondary text line, in two states only: `yourImports.publicWriting` ("Writing the public version") for `pending` and `writing`, and `yourImports.publicReady` ("Public version ready to read") for `ready`. It wraps to 2 lines without truncating, and web and mobile are the same. A file import is one grouped row for the whole file, the existing To review row with no new component: the title is the file name, the status line under it in secondary text, and a trailing chevron. The status line shows these segments in this order, only those above 0, joined with `Intl.ListFormat` (style narrow, type unit) and never a hand-written separator: `yourImports.fileExpiring`, `yourImports.fileNeedsYou`, `yourImports.fileReady`, `yourImports.fileWriting`, `yourImports.fileNotWritten`, `yourImports.filePublished`. Queued drafts that wait for writer capacity count as writing. `yourImports.fileNotWritten` reads "not saved yet". Recipes with 7 days or fewer left before the purge read `yourImports.fileExpiring` ("will be removed on {date}") instead of "not saved yet", where `{date}` is the earliest purge date among them, shown as a day with no time (a purge date already in the past shows nothing for that recipe, because it is about to go). With needs-you or expiring above 0 that segment uses the warning tone with a leading `alert` glyph (16), and the rest stays secondary. On mobile it is a full-width row, the whole row is the target, and the line may wrap to 2 lines at 320 and never truncates a count. A tap opens the file's item list as today. The row leaves To review only when the file has no unsaved, undiscarded recipe, never because the counts are all 0. With every count at 0 the status line is empty and the row shows the file name alone. After "Make the rest public" the button area becomes an inline summary: `restPublic.started`, then each skip line above 0 (`restPublic.skip.publicExists`, `.budget`, `.attemptsUsed`, `.waiting`, and `.offerChanged` after them), all in secondary tone, then Done (`failed.done`). There is no snackbar, and the started line is announced once in a polite live region. If 0 start, the started line is dropped and only the skip lines and Done show. The reasons `alreadyStarted`, `saved` and `discarded` show no line. Recipes skipped as `offerChanged` count as "not saved yet" (`yourImports.fileNotWritten`) on the grouped row.

Attempts are counted by a holder, 3 in total: the import item while the job is a For everyone job, and the saved recipe for a Both job and the later route (the item's count is copied into the recipe when it is saved). Reopening and starting a new job does not reset them, and editing the private copy never does. With 3 used, "For everyone" and "Both" answer `409 attemptsUsed` and the cook sees `failed.titleFinal` and `failed.bodyFinal`, and the private exit (the wording of `review.notNow`) runs the paste's ordinary Save recipe. With no free slot the body is `failed.bodyFinalFull` and the only exit is `review.discard`, which calls the existing import delete, `DELETE /api/v1/import/{importId}`, because no job is live. (Ruled 2026-11-19.) On a file item the private exit is the wizard's per-recipe decision write and then advance, and with no free slot the body is `failed.bodyFinalFullFile` with `review.discardFile`. An import discarded with Stop or `review.discard` is deleted and not listed.

## 9. Accessibility contract (WCAG 2.2 AA)

- **Headings.** One H1 per screen (P5, P7, P8, P9). In P5 the visibility area's title is H2. In P8 "Steps", "Public
  version", "Your copy" and the flags title are H2. The plan name is H3.
- **The choice.** A radio group named by its H2 (`aria-labelledby`), with the explain text as its description. Each
  radio's accessible name is its label, and its hint is its description. Arrow keys move and select on web.
- **Continue with nothing chosen (3.3.1).** The group gets `aria-invalid` and the `pickOne` alert in
  `aria-describedby`. Focus moves to the group's heading.
- **Reading order (1.3.2, 2.4.3).** P8 two columns: DOM header, name, side, steps (§3.3).
- **Status (4.1.3).** `writing.announce`, the `done.*` lines, `pro.welcome` and `deadEnd.closed` go through one polite
  `LiveRegion`. Refusals, failures, offline and flags are `role="alert"`.
- **Glyphs.** The writing state uses a still glyph, never a spinner (2.2.2).
- **Disclosure.** "Show my copy" is a button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
- **Prices.** The figure group has the accessible name `pro.price`, so a screen reader reads one sentence, not four
  fragments.
- **Targets (2.5.8).** Radio rows at least 52 tall and full width. Buttons 44 tall on web, 44 pt on iOS, 48 dp on
  Android. Links in text have 24 px of line height.
- **Focus not obscured (2.4.11).** The pinned bars reserve their height under a focused field.
- **Contrast (1.4.3, 1.4.11).** `actionText` on `selectedFill` (badge), `danger` outline on `paperRaised`, `inkMuted`
  hints: the designer looked at them in light and dark on the boards.
- **Age question.** A modal dialog that traps focus, lands on its title and returns focus to Publish.
- **Voice control (2.5.3).** Every visible label is in its accessible name.
- **Reduced motion.** No motion of our own.

## 11. Edge cases

- **Longest content.** §4's table.
- **Pro cook.** No slot lines anywhere. Never a dead end (Pro has no limit).
- **One slot left.** `private.hint` and `both.hint` use the ICU `one` form.
- **No slot left and a public version possible.** `publicOnly` (B4), never a dead end.
- **The offer changes between read and press.** `409 offerChanged` (B8).
- **Another device publishes first.** `409 publicExists`: the area re-renders as `publicExists.published`.
- **Retry at 3 attempts.** `409 attemptsUsed`: the client refetches the job and re-renders from its state and attempts. The public path's private exit (`review.notNow`, `button.private`) calls the keepPrivate route, and with no free slot shows the no-slot set (`review.discard`, which calls cancel; on a file item `review.discardFile`, which calls the discard-item route). At 3 attempts the screens hide `wizard.retry` and `flag.rewrite` and show `blocked.bodyFinal` or `blocked.bodyFinalBoth` where blocked, and the failed Final variants where failed: `failed.titleFinal` with `failed.bodyFinal` and `review.notNow` on the public path, `failed.bodyFinalBoth` and `failed.done` on the Both path (B8).
- **The private copy changes during writing (Both).** `409 privateCopyChanged` at Publish: `flag.fix` with "Write it
  again".
- **Under 16 learned at the age question.** "Not now" publishes nothing. The cook can still keep a private copy from P8
  (`review.notNow`; on the Both path the copy is already saved).
- **App killed during writing.** The job carries on. For everyone: the import waits under "To review in My recipes" and reopens by §6.11. Both: your copy is in My recipes, and its page shows the public version's progress (`yourImports.publicWriting` or `yourImports.publicReady`, which opens P7 or P8). After the job's 24-hour expiry the later route's "Share a public version" stays. The line sits in the P6 credit block (§3.5).
- **Long currencies and prices.** §4.
- **Trial eligibility unknown** (the store did not answer). Hide the badge and `pro.trial`. Never promise a trial that
  the payment page can refuse.
