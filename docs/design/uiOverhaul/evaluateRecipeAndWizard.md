# UI overhaul: evaluation of recipe detail, versions, wizard, ingredient rows, paste flow

⛔ **DESIGN EVALUATION. NOT PRODUCTION CODE.** It reports findings and changes no code. The design that fixes them
is `specRecipeAndWizard.md`, beside this file.

- **Mode:** EVALUATE. **Date:** 2026-10-08. **Agent:** `staff-ux-engineer`.
- **Brief (owner, verbatim):** "Every where on all devices. Things are misaligned, don't even look close to the
  mockups, incorrect sizes, unreadable, wraps when it shouldn't and incorrectly (remember, wrapping is the second
  to last resort to fit stuff)… It looks like shit."
- **Standing.** I wrote earlier specs for the ingredient row (`docs/design/ingredientStatusExplanation.md`,
  `rowEditorOpenDecisions.md`, `ingredientSpecialization.md`). Several defects below come from those specs, not
  from how they were built. So I judge against outside criteria where I can: WCAG 2.2 AA by number, Nielsen's 10,
  the mockups, and the owner's wrap rule.
- **Seen rendered:** yes, web. 135 production-build captures (27 states × 320/390/768/1280/1920). I cut each into
  viewport-height tiles to read it, and compared the menu, delete and open-list states pixel by pixel
  against the base captures. Mockups: I rendered `docs/mockups/screens/screenRecipeDetail.html` at 390 and 1280
  myself. Native: partly. Of the 16 Pixel 6 captures, only step 1 and the discard dialog show my surfaces (by md5,
  `recipeDetail*.png` match `discover`/`profile`, `step2_empty` matches `step1_bottom`, and `step2_row` shows step 1).
  The other native findings come from source and are marked **(source)**.
- **Not user evidence.** These findings come from inspection. No user was observed.
- **Severity** (`critique-handoff.md`): 4 = must fix before release · 3 major · 2 minor · 1 cosmetic.
- **Capture artifacts I excluded:** fixed bars that appear mid-page in stitched captures. I judged overlaps only
  inside the first viewport height. The "2 cup" on every parse-review line, the `-4 tbsp iced water` name and the
  `g cold unsalted butter` name all come from the capture mock (`web/tests/e2e/utils/recipeApi.ts:1048-1056`), so
  they are not reported as bugs.

## Criteria

User: a home cook. Tasks: read and cook from a recipe. Enter or fix a recipe across four steps. Paste a list.
Context: a phone in the kitchen (often one hand, at arm's length), and a laptop for longer entry. Measured against:
WCAG 2.2 AA. Nielsen's 10. The mockups (`screenRecipeDetail.html`, the Figma Make `IngredientsStep.tsx` and its
two briefs, the 001 wireframes `recipe-detail.md`/`recipe-edit.md`/`version-history.md`). The owner's wrap rule
(memory `wrappingInControlElements`: wrap only as a second-to-last resort, and never inside a short control). And
`@commise/ui` tokens.

## Passes run

- **Heuristic (Nielsen 10):** above. Most frequent: #8 (aesthetic and minimalist), #4 (consistency), #6 (recognition).
- **WCAG 2.2 AA, by number:** 1.3.1 and 3.3.2 **fail** (R2, I3, unlabelled range field). 1.4.3: slate on pearl 4.81:1
  and slate on white 5.24:1 pass (computed). Placeholder ≈2.9:1 (sampled) is noted under R2, but WCAG's coverage of
  placeholder text is debated, and the binding failure is the missing label. 1.4.10: no horizontal page scroll seen
  at 320, but content is clipped or overlapped (I1, D5, R6). 1.4.11: coral outlines are 2.4:1, but the buttons carry
  text, so no boundary contrast is required. No failure. 2.4.11: D2 and R7 (R7 is exempt as user-opened content
  per V3-M1, D2 is a menu the user opened too, also exempt, but still severity 3 under Nielsen #3). 2.5.3: the short
  labels "Prev"/"Save" are contained in their names (pass). 2.5.7: no reorder exists for steps or rows (I4), so no
  drag-only failure. 2.5.8: targets 44 px (pass). 3.3.1/3.3.3 **fail** (W4: the error is not at its field). 4.1.2:
  D3's "menu" is not a menu (fail as `role` mismatch on native `accessibilityRole="menu"` over buttons, source).
- **States:** detail own/other/menu/delete, versions empty, wizard empty/invalid/filled/each step, every row state,
  each open list, paste empty/filled/review were seen. **Not seen:** versions populated/compare/preview, conflict,
  the paste review's partial/failed/expired states, the offline notice, the detail loading/error states, the delete
  in flight.
- **Cross-platform (ten points):** 1 web phone/tablet/desktop + iOS/Android named. 2 dispositions: none stated
  anywhere today. 3 primary action: wizard Next is bottom-right below `lg` (good), but detail has no thumb-zone
  action. 4 hover: the row's ⋮ is visible (good). 5 longest string: fails (R2, R8, W1, F4, F10). 6 at 320: clipping (I1,
  D5, R6). 7 scroll budget: fails (R1, D4). 8 keyboard open: not checkable from captures. 9 back: two Backs on detail
  (D7). 10 rule placement: N1, R2 and W1 are primitive defects.
- **Content:** "Resolution failed" (jargon), "Prev" (abbreviation), Title Case drift, "16200s timer" (F1).
- **Deceptive-pattern screen:** run. The premium upsell in the More menu is informative, declinable and not
  pre-selected. It passes the DSA Art. 25 test. None found.
