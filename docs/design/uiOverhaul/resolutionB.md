# UI overhaul: resolution B, proposal A against proposal B

⛔ **DESIGN RESOLUTION. NOT PRODUCTION CODE.** It compares `proposalA.md` with my `proposalB.md`, point by
point, and gives a verdict on each. I did not read `resolutionA.md`.

- **Date:** 2026-10-08. **Agent:** `staff-ux-engineer`, the author of proposal B. So this is partly a
  judgement on my own work. To offset that bias, I checked A's factual claims against the repo before
  ruling, and I concede wherever A's evidence holds.
- **Verdicts:**
    - **ADOPT A:** A's position replaces mine.
    - **KEEP B:** my position stands, with a reason in evidence or a named principle.
    - **MERGE:** a combined rule, stated exactly.
    - **OWNER:** the owner must decide. My recommendation is in §3.
- **A's claims I checked today, and what I found:**
    - `specs/008-cooking-mode/spec.md` exists, with status Draft, and FR-035 says "System MUST keep the
      device screen active while Cooking Mode is engaged". **True.** My proposal said the cook view needed
      a new FR. That was wrong.
    - Figma brief 1 says "A cook never types a free-form ingredient line". **True** (brief line 48). My
      one-line entry committed an unmatched line "as written", which breaks this.
    - `recipe-import-core`'s full parser must stay outside the Expo bundle. **True**:
      `ingredientLine.ts` says recipe-core "ships in the Expo bundle, which is the boundary
      `corpusPipeline.integration.test.ts` asserts this package stays outside of". My mechanism for one-line
      entry is not buildable as I wrote it.
    - FR-005b: a copy needs a substantive edit before it can be published. **True.**
    - `recipe-search.md:74`: "Clone button: Available on all public recipe cards (FR-005)". **True.** So
      A's removal of the card button departs from a wireframe, as A itself says.
- **Two A positions conflict with recorded rulings:**
    - A paints the beach-glow wash only behind sign-in and the 404. `globals.css` paints it on `body` on
      purpose (issue #145).
    - On an offline recipe page, A labels edit actions "Needs a connection". The owner's offline directive
      (memory `offlineLocalFirstArchitecture`) says consumers never branch on connectivity.

## §2. Points where A and B already agree

- Coral leaves every control. Secondary buttons are neutral: white, pewter edge, charcoal text.
- One primary seafoam action per view.
- "Community" becomes "Discover", and Discover is a top-level destination.
- Collections sits beside My recipes inside Recipes.
- Unbuilt features appear in no navigation. They live only as Home's FR-046 placeholders.
- No drawer on phone web. No bell. No top-bar magnifier. Search lives in the page it searches.
- No top bar on desktop. The H1 sits in the content, and the avatar or profile row sits at the sidebar foot.
- Large visible titles, with no hidden H1 or second title.
- The native tab bar stays on pushed screens and hides for modal tasks.
- `content-wide` becomes 1440.
- Playfair sets names only. Numbers are Inter with tabular figures.
- Lucide on both platforms.
- The first audit's tokens stay: `pewter`, the difficulty tint and meter, native Inter, the Android elevation map.
- The first audit's primitives stay: `Chip`, `ChipRow`, `SearchField`, `UndoSnackbar`, `FieldLabel`, `DurationField`, `Stepper`, `formatDuration`.
- The detail hero is the carousel, with the cover shown once.
- Back and ⋯ sit on the hero as white 44 px discs.
- Serves sits in the Ingredients heading, because it scales those amounts.
- A sticky jump bar (Ingredients, Steps or Method, Nutrition) on long reading pages.
- The phone description is clamped to 4 lines with "More".
- Ingredient rows are tap-to-check, and the checks last for the session.
- Edit can open the editor at the section the cook was looking at.
- Read rows with one open editor. Healthy rows show no status glyph.
- Groups are set at the section level, and rows move with "Move to group…".
- No invented "1" for an amount.
- "Paste a list" sits inside the Ingredients section and feeds the same parse job.
- Preview opens the real detail page in a sheet.
- Version restore makes a new version, with undo.
- The collection picker is multi-select, with a visible count.
- Removing a recipe from a collection is undo, not a dialog.
- Profile, Settings and Account become one Profile page.
- Sign-up asks for email and password (plus Google), with no username or names.
- Title cap 120 as a soft limit (O1).
- "Clone" becomes "Save a copy" (O5).
- The phone row card defers tags and version only (Q9). The grid keeps the CR-002 set.
- The first audit's rail skeleton fix (E1) ships first.
