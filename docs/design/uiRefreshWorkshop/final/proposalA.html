Commise · UI refresh · Final proposal A · led by Designer 1

Still Life, lit

The app becomes a calm room of plaster, paper and one seafoam green, and every bit of colour comes from the food. A recipe with no photo gets an overhead still life of its own ingredients, and that food’s colour softly lights the page it lives on.

A sample, not the full set. Every screen keeps today’s layout, order and copy. Only the material changes: colour, type, surfaces, shape, icons, pictures and motion.

Three screens, in daylight

Home takes the light of its first recent recipe, so this morning it is the pancakes’ butter. My recipes becomes a wall of pictures of the cook’s own food, with two unfinished studies waiting to be coloured in. The recipe page is lit by its own cover: the roast chicken’s terra glow at the top, and again on the step being cooked.

gluten-free · dinner · sunday roast

Nutrition
612 cal per serving

Ingredients

Edit
Servings
4
Chicken
1 whole chickenabout 1.8 kg
3 tbsp unsalted buttersoftened
1 lemonzested and halved
6 sprigs fresh thyme
1 head garlichalved crosswise
1 tsp flaky sea salt
Vegetables
800 g waxy potatoesquartered
3 carrotscut into chunks
2 red onionscut into wedges
2 tbsp olive oil

Steps

1

Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Take the chicken out of the fridge, pat the skin dry with paper towels, and leave it at room temperature while the oven heats.

2

Mash the butter with the lemon zest, half of the thyme leaves and the salt. Work your fingers under the skin of the breast and spread the butter over the meat.

3

Scatter the potatoes, carrots and onions in a large roasting tin and toss with the olive oil. Put the chicken on top with the halved lemon, the garlic and the rest of the thyme in the cavity.

4

Roast for 20 minutes, then lower the oven to 190°C (170°C fan) and roast for another 1 hour 10 minutes, until the juices run clear.

1 h 30 min
5

Rest the chicken, loosely covered with foil, for 15 minutes before carving. Spoon the pan juices over the vegetables.

15 min

After hours

Dark is warm brown-black, not grey. The food keeps its colour from a real dark swatch for every fill, with no filter anywhere. Plates step down so the food is still the brightest thing in the frame, and the status bar is chosen from the ground of the active theme.

Motion

One signature motion: the check toggle, 220 ms, a small overshoot (scale 0.85 → 1.06 → 1). Everything else eases out and stops. Opening a recipe grows the card’s picture into the hero (280 ms) and the recipe’s light blooms down the page (200 ms); the whole open ends by 380 ms. Nothing loops, nothing follows the scroll. With reduced motion, every one of these is an instant change.
