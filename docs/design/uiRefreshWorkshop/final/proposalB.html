Kitchen Press

Every recipe owns a small print of its own foods, pulled in two inks on coloured paper, with the second plate a hair out of register so a third, named colour appears where they cross. Everything around the prints stays flat, warm and quiet: one green means “press”, and colour never leaves the picture.

How each print was chosen

  • Lemon ricotta pancakes. Pancakes are named last, so they are plate A, in ochre. Ochre pairs only with tomato, so the lemon prints in tomato. Rust where they cross.
  • Weeknight pad thai. Rice noodles have no ink row, so the seed picks tomato. Coriander is plate B, in herb. Umber where they cross.
  • Roast tomato soup. The title names tomato: a halved fruit, in tomato. The onion is plate B. The seed chose sea, the pair least used in Eliza’s last eight. Aubergine where they cross.
  • Spaghetti alla carbonara. Spaghetti is pasta, so ochre. Guanciale is plate B, in tomato.
  • Shakshuka with feta. The only food the title names is feta: dairy, so ochre. Its tomatoes are plate B.
  • Chickpea and spinach curry. Spinach is named last, so it is plate A, in herb. Chickpeas are plate B, in beet. Moss where they cross.
  • Miso butter mushroom ramen. Ramen are wheat noodles, so ochre. The mushrooms are plate B, in tomato.
  • Red lentil dal with crispy shallots. Red lentils have no ink row, so the seed picks tomato. The crispy shallots are plate B, in herb.
  • Brown butter chocolate chip cookies. Cookies are baking, so ochre. The chips are plate B in tomato. They sit on the cookies, so they print as rust, the colour of chocolate.

Lemon and thyme roast chicken

PublicSource: Family recipe cardVersion history
1 h 50 minTotal
20 minPrep
MediumDifficulty
Edit recipe

A Sunday roast that does the work for you: butter, lemon and thyme worked under the skin, and everything else cooking in the same tin so the vegetables catch the juices. Crisp skin, soft potatoes, and a pan sauce that needs no extra effort.

gluten-free · dinner · sunday roast

Ingredients

for 4
4
Chicken
1 whole chickenabout 1.8 kg
3 tbsp unsalted buttersoftened
1 lemonzested and halved
6 sprigs fresh thyme
1 head garlichalved crosswise
1 tsp flaky sea salt
Vegetables
800 g waxy potatoesquartered
3 carrotscut into chunks
2 red onionscut into wedges
2 tbsp olive oil
Freshly ground black pepperto taste

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Take the chicken out of the fridge, pat the skin dry with paper towels, and leave it at room temperature while the oven heats.

  2. Mash the butter with the lemon zest, half of the thyme leaves and the salt. Work your fingers under the skin of the breast and spread the butter over the meat.

  3. Scatter the potatoes, carrots and onions in a large roasting tin and toss with the olive oil. Put the chicken on top with the halved lemon, the garlic and the rest of the thyme in the cavity.

  4. Roast for 20 minutes, then lower the oven to 190°C (170°C fan) and roast for another 1 hour 10 minutes, until the juices run clear.

    1 h 30 min

  5. Rest the chicken, loosely covered with foil, for 15 minutes before carving. Spoon the pan juices over the vegetables.

    15 min

What changes at night

  • The ground. Five card stocks with real hue, 1.32 to 1.86:1 against dark paper, so a cover keeps its edge.
  • The inks. Mid-tones, 2.50 to 3.70:1 on their grounds, so nothing glows.
  • The overlaps. They go lighter than both parents, not darker. Rust becomes crust, a toasted gold.
  • The grain. Bipolar: as many specks darker than the ground as lighter, so on OLED it reads as paper, not dust.
  • The action. #7CC8BE with a near-black label at 9.20:1.
  • The current step. A light disc with a dark numeral, on sheet, with the action rule.

The registration mark

A photo recipe still owns a print. Its mark sits beside the overline: plate A and plate B, 2.5 px out, the overlap lens between. It is also the brand mark.

Italian

The same mark, enlarged: ochre, tomato and the rust lens, from the focaccia’s hidden print.

Collections are overprints

“Weeknight dinners”: plate A of its first three recipes, each in its own ink, with every pairwise overlap. Two are photo recipes giving their hidden print.

Beet: Chicken Cacciatore · tomato: Miso-Glazed Salmon · herb: Tomato Basil Soup. Garnet, moss and umber where they cross.

Motion

Nothing moves on first view, on scroll or on refresh. Two moments move, and only after the cook acts:

  • The check (the signature): 220 ms, cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1), 0.85 → 1.06 → 1. The box fills with action; the strike draws in inkMuted.
  • The stamp (owner call): once, after publish. Plate B starts 6 px out and lands at 2.5 px on the same curve.

With reduced motion on, both show their end state at once. The grain never moves.
