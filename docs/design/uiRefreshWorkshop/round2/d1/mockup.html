Lemon and thyme roast chicken

PublicSource: Family recipe cardVersion history
1 h 50 minTotal
20 minPrep
MediumDifficulty
Edit recipe
Screen on

A Sunday roast that does the work for you: butter, lemon and thyme worked under the skin, and everything else cooking in the same tin so the vegetables catch the juices. Crisp skin, soft potatoes, and a pan sauce that needs…

More

gluten-free · dinner · sunday roast

Nutrition
612 cal per serving

Ingredients

Edit
Servings
4
Chicken
1 whole chickenabout 1.8 kg
3 tbsp unsalted buttersoftened
1 lemonzested and halved
6 sprigs fresh thyme
1 head garlichalved crosswise
1 tsp flaky sea salt
Vegetables
800 g waxy potatoesquartered
3 carrotscut into chunks
2 red onionscut into wedges
2 tbsp olive oil

Steps

1

Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Take the chicken out of the fridge, pat the skin dry with paper towels, and leave it at room temperature while the oven heats.

2
Now

Mash the butter with the lemon zest, half of the thyme leaves and the salt. Work your fingers under the skin of the breast and spread the butter over the meat.

3

Scatter the potatoes, carrots and onions in a large roasting tin and toss with the olive oil. Put the chicken on top with the halved lemon, the garlic and the rest of the thyme in the cavity.

4

Roast for 20 minutes, then lower the oven to 190°C (170°C fan) and roast for another 1 hour 10 minutes, until the juices run clear.

Twelve covers, one parts library

Real Discover titles at 160 px (the phone 2 × 2 size), small level of detail on. Each is ground + vessel + dish form + hero + 1–3 accents. The hero is the most salient matched food: skip the 30 staples (onion, garlic, oil, butter, salt, pepper, flour, sugar, water, stock…), then take the food rarest in this cook's library. The vessel and dish form come from the title and tags. The layout comes from a hash of the recipe id. Edge cases are tagged.
