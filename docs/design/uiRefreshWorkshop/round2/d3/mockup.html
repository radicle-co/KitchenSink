Mise en Place

A recipe with no photo gets a plate: its first three matched ingredients, laid out on a coloured ground the way a commis sets out a station before service. The plate never draws the finished dish, so the generator never has to invent one. Each ground also gives the recipe page a quiet key tone for the words you read. Everything you press stays basil. The chrome stays oat paper and green-black ink. At night the grounds go deep, and the food sits under a warm lamp.

Layouts, flows and copy are the app’s own boards. Only the visual language changes.

Lemon and thyme roast chicken

Public Source: Family recipe card Version history
1 h 50 minTotal
20 minPrep
 MediumDifficulty
Edit recipe
Screen onOff

A Sunday roast that does the work for you: butter, lemon and thyme worked under the skin, and everything else cooking in the same tin so the vegetables catch the juices. Crisp skin, soft potatoes, and a pan sauce that needs no extra effort.

More

gluten-free · dinner · sunday roast

Nutrition612 cal per serving

Ingredients

for 4 4
Chicken
  • 1 whole chicken
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 6 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 tsp flaky sea salt
Vegetables
  • 800 g waxy potatoes
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 red onions
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Steps

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Take the chicken out of the fridge, pat the skin dry with paper towels, and leave it at room temperature while the oven heats.

  2. 2

    Mash the butter with the lemon zest, half of the thyme leaves and the salt. Work your fingers under the skin of the breast and spread the butter over the meat.

  3. 3

    Scatter the potatoes, carrots and onions in a large roasting tin and toss with the olive oil. Put the chicken on top with the halved lemon, the garlic and the rest of the thyme in the cavity.

  4. 4

    Roast for 20 minutes, then lower the oven to 190°C (170°C fan) and roast for another 1 hour 10 minutes, until the juices run clear.

    1 h 30 min
  5. 5

    Rest the chicken, loosely covered with foil, for 15 minutes before carving. Spoon the pan juices over the vegetables.

    15 min

The plate rule, applied literally

Every recipe plate in this file is placed by this rule in the generator script, with no hand fixes (the sidebar brand mark is a fixed asset). Hero: the first matched ingredient with a motif, centred. 2nd and 3rd: the next two, in seeded corners. Accent: a neutral (whisk, spoon, cloth, fork, crumbs), never food.

plate.ground and plate.key · light / dark

Grounds never carry text. Each ground has a key: a text-safe tone (light: at least 4.9:1 on canvas, paper and the tinted wash; dark: at least 8.3:1) for words you read on that recipe’s page, never for anything you press. Tomato borrows chili’s key, so no key is ever red: red belongs to danger.
