Round 3 makes the covers vary (five composition families), makes the recipe’s light visible and safe (OKLCH, fixed lightness), quiets the current step, and turns the fallback cover into an unfinished study that ripens as the cook matches ingredients.

Ingredients

Servings
4
Chicken
1 whole chickenabout 1.8 kg
3 tbsp unsalted buttersoftened
1 lemonzested and halved
6 sprigs fresh thyme
1 head garlichalved crosswise
1 tsp flaky sea salt
Vegetables
800 g waxy potatoesquartered
3 carrotscut into chunks
2 red onionscut into wedges
2 tbsp olive oil

Steps

1

Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Take the chicken out of the fridge, pat the skin dry with paper towels, and leave it at room temperature while the oven heats.

2

Mash the butter with the lemon zest, half of the thyme leaves and the salt. Work your fingers under the skin of the breast and spread the butter over the meat.

3

Scatter the potatoes, carrots and onions in a large roasting tin and toss with the olive oil. Put the chicken on top with the halved lemon, the garlic and the rest of the thyme in the cavity.

4

Roast for 20 minutes, then lower the oven to 190°C (170°C fan) and roast for another 1 hour 10 minutes, until the juices run clear.

1 h 30 min
5

Rest the chicken, loosely covered with foil, for 15 minutes before carving. Spoon the pan juices over the vegetables.

15 min

Same strength, every recipe. Each wash is the cover’s hue at OKLCH L 0.944, with the chroma solved so it lands ΔE 6.1 to 9.1 from plaster. Round 2’s mixes ranged from ΔE 2.8 (invisible) to a failing grey. Because lightness is fixed, inkMuted (4.90 to 4.98), actionText (5.20 to 5.30) and the honey stars (3.72 to 3.79) pass on every hue without a guard.

Photos. At upload we sample the photo once: its dominant hue gives the wash, and the luminance of its top 96 px gives statusBar. The bread’s top band gets dark status text: ink is 7.0 on the crust and 5.53 on the darkest dimple. No scrim is needed when the sample exists; the fallback scrim is in concept.md.

Every outlined control keeps its own paper fill. Pewter on the wash is only 3.11 to 3.16, so a bare edge on the wash is never allowed.

The generator, light and dark

Every cover is built with <use> from one parts library. The hash picks one of five families (centred, cropped, close-up, pair, pattern) and one of four hero slots, and the hero is drawn in the dish. Tomato soup and gazpacho now sit side by side as different pictures. A recipe with fewer than two matched foods is an unfinished study: a pewter line drawing on paper in the recipe’s own light. Shown at the real phone card size, with small marks dropped.
