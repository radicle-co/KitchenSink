The Now Line

Every screen is drawn across one line of light that never moves, set at eye height: what you have done sits above it, what is ahead sits below, and whatever crosses it is lit, large and readable from the stove. It is a teleprompter for cooking, the brand mark and the map of time in one stroke, and each dish brings its own colour to it.

These phones are real. Scroll inside any of them: the item that crosses the line grows and inks in. On a recipe, the “Next step” button moves the next step onto the line.
