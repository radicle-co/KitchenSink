Commise UI refresh · Proposal B

The Table

Commise, seen from directly above, the way every cooking video films a kitchen: the screen is a table, each recipe is a plate on it, and a bigger plate takes longer to cook. The plate's rim is a dial you turn to move through the steps, your ingredients wait in bowls you empty as you go, and at night the table becomes black glass lit from below by whatever you cooked last.

Prep timeCook timeRest timeNotches: steps

Light: the enamel table

A bright enamel-blue tabletop, white plates, and real overhead shadows. There are no cards and no rows. Colour appears only as food and as time on the rims.

Dark: night is the hob

The table becomes black glass. Nothing casts a shadow and nothing glows, except the plate you cooked last night: light comes up through the glass from underneath it, and fades over a few days.

One camera, real objects

Everything is seen from above and is something a cook already owns: plate, bowl, board, tray, cloche. Nothing is a card.

Size is time

Plate area follows total time, so a slow Sunday roast is visibly bigger than 25-minute pancakes before you read a word.

Turn the dish to cook it

The rim is a real slider with five values. Arrow keys and the Next button do the same thing, so the dial is never the only way.

Colour is never alone

Every bowl shows its quantity and name; the warm plate also says “Cooked last night”. All text meets WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes.
