# Round 1 critique, Designer 1

## The headline: we three made the same design

Mine ("The Recipe Is a Clock"), D2's "Induction" and D3's "Plate
Signatures" make one claim. There are no photos. A recipe is a **ring glyph**. Its arcs are step times, coloured
by heat. It sits on a cool ground, under a grotesque display face (D3 and I chose the same one, Bricolage), with
one slow "breathing" motion. The owner rejected the last round because two finals looked alike. Now three do. That
is the main finding of this round, and it is on all of us, me included.

We share one blind spot too: **no food appears anywhere.** People open a recipe app partly from appetite. A ring of
arcs reads as a fitness tracker (Apple Activity rings, Oura), not as dinner. The brief also keeps a hero on Recipe
detail. Where a cook has a photo, we owe it a place.

## D2: Induction

- **New or disguised?** Light coming up _through_ hob glass is new. The ring itself is Apple Fitness in kitchen
  colours.
- **Excites?** Yes, on dark. Light mode loses the idea, because a hob only glows in the dark.
- **Calm and easy?** Yes. "One light source per screen" is a good rule.
- **Keep:** "Coming soon" as **unlit burners**. It explains the roadmap with no words. Best idea of the round.
- **Must change:** (1) Give light mode its own idea, not a dimmer dark. (2) Five heat hues plus violet "rest" is a
  lot to learn. (3) A "plain, quiet" ingredient list gives up on the screen a cook reads most.
- **Bold push:** make the ring the **control**. Turn it like a hob knob to move through steps, and let the lit arc
  be the timer.

## D3: Plate Signatures

- **New or disguised?** The richest glyph: time ring, step ticks, ingredient seeds by food family. **Size equals
  total time** on Home is a real idea. But the frame is the old shape: white rounded cards, pill buttons, uppercase
  labels. It is a card app with a better thumbnail.
- **Excites?** Less than it can. On light grey it reads as a data report.
- **Calm and easy?** The calmest and clearest. "Nothing set on a curve" is wise.
- **Keep:** seeds coloured by food family and tied to each ingredient line. Only D3 links the picture to the list.
- **Must change:** (1) Drop the cards. Put the plates on the ground at true size, as on a table. (2) Nine food
  hues is too many for a calm screen. (3) The hero legend repeats the facts strip in a new form.
- **Bold push:** show a Collection as a **table seen from above**, with plates that overlap. No app has that view.

## How we differ

- D2 is light and heat (feeling). D3 is parts and structure (information). Mine is time as the layout (Home as a
  time river) with colour orbs made from ingredients.
- All three use the ring as the face of a recipe. With that much overlap, three directions give the owner one
  choice.

**Proposal for round 2:** split the ground on purpose. D2 keeps the ring and the light, its strongest form. D3 keeps
the seeds and the table layout, without cards. I drop the dial and take a direction with **no ring**: real food
images and colour as light and depth, set out along time. Each of us shows where real food images live, so no
design reads as a dashboard.
