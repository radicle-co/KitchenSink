# Round 1 critique, Designer 2

## The headline: we made one design three times

The owner said last round's finals "look nearly identical". This round, all three of us drew **the same idea**. Each recipe is a generated ring of its own time. The ground is dark and cool. There are no
photos. The type is a quirky grotesque (Bricolage or Sora) over Inter.
D1 calls it a clock, D3 a plate, I called it a hob. The owner will see one direction with three names. That is my
fault as much as anyone's. Round 2 must split us on purpose.

It also resembles something: a watch complication, Apple Fitness rings, a Spotify Wrapped data glyph. New for
recipes, familiar as a look.

Two shared risks:

1. **No food.** People choose what to cook with their appetite, and appetite is visual. A glyph can stand in when there is no
   photo. It cannot replace one. Recipe detail must lead with the hero photo when it exists.
2. **Data we do not have.** Steps carry only an optional `timerSeconds`. There is no heat per step and no duration
   on most steps. D1's mint-to-ember arcs and my heat colours are guesses dressed as facts. D3's prep/cook/rest
   arcs come from real fields.

## D1, "The Recipe Is a Clock"

- **New?** Partly. The time river on Home is the most original layout idea of the three.
- **Excites?** Yes. The orbs are lovely.
- **Calm?** Least calm of the three: auras, blur, glass tab bar, a gradient title. It drifts toward a crypto-app look.
- **Keep:** the "now" line. Time as the axis of Home, with what is coming above and what you cooked below.
- **Change:** (1) Drop step heat colour, which we cannot source. (2) Cut the blur and aura layers to one light
  source. (3) Let photos into the orbs: a photo tinted into the flavour orb.
- **Push:** make the now line literal. At 5 pm, Home leans toward dinner.

## D3, "Plate Signatures"

- **New?** The most rigorous, every mark is real data. But the screen is still a list of rows with a mark
  on the left.
- **Excites?** Less than D1.
- **Calm?** The calmest. Solid text, no glow, big 48 px controls.
- **Keep:** **size is total time** on Home, so quick and slow read before any words. That is the best single idea
  in the round.
- **Change:** (1) Break the row list. Let signatures sit at their real sizes in open space, like plates on a table.
  (2) The ingredient seeds are too small to read on a phone. At 390 px, rings plus seeds is too much detail.
  (3) Give it one moment of delight, such as the ring filling as you tick steps off.
- **Push:** the collection as a table of overlapping plates. Build it.

## Where we differ

D1 is about **light**, D3 about **information**, mine about **material** (hob glass, with "Soon" features as unlit
burners). The ground, type, ring and "no photos" stance are shared by all three. Those are the overlaps to break.

## Proposal for round 2

Keep one ring direction. **D3 carries it**, because it has the honest data, and borrows D1's now line.
The other two of us leave the ring behind completely:

- One takes **food as the image**: full-bleed photography and colour as material, not data glyphs.
- One takes **space and motion**: depth, layers, how a screen opens.

No two designers share a ground colour, a display face, or a hero device.
