# Round 1 critique (Designer 3)

## The headline: all three of us drew the same thing

D1's dial, D2's heat signature and my plate signature are one idea: **a ring of arcs**. Arc length is step time
and colour is heat. All three sit on a cool dark ground, with Bricolage or Sora over Inter. The owner called the
last two finals nearly identical. Three rings repeat that failure one level up. Round 2 must split us apart
on purpose. I propose: D2 keeps the ring, D1 trades it for the time river, and I trade it for something else.

## D1: "The Recipe Is a Clock"

- **New or disguised?** Half new. The time river on Home is new. The dial is an Apple Watch activity ring, and
  dark navy with mint-to-ember reads as a smart-home dashboard.
- **Excites?** The flavour-fingerprint orbs do. They are the most food-like idea here.
- **Calm?** Mostly. The cooking view packs a dial, tick labels, a timer, ingredients and two
  buttons. That is too busy for a stove.
- **Keep:** the time river (a "now" line, ahead above, past below) and the orbs.
- **Change, in order:** 1) Drop the dial. Let the river and the orbs be the whole identity. 2) Remove the
  tick numbers. Nobody reads minute 23 off a ring. 3) Cut the glow magenta.
- **Bold push:** make the river the whole app. Discover, Recipes and Collections are the same surface at other
  time scales (this week, this season, ever), and a pinch moves between them.

## D2: "Induction"

- **New or disguised?** The most coherent metaphor. "Coming soon" as unlit burners is an excellent wordless
  roadmap. But it looks most like a sci-fi concept shot, and light mode loses the idea: light from below only
  shows in the dark.
- **Excites?** In dark, yes. In light, it is a plain list with coloured rings.
- **Calm?** Yes. One light source, one slow 6 s breath.
- **Keep:** the unlit burners, and the step list where each step shows its own arc.
- **Change, in order:** 1) Give light mode its own material (frosted ceramic, light as soft shadow), not dark
  with the colours flipped. 2) Recent recipes under the hero ring fall back to a plain row list. Lay them out as
  burners of different sizes. 3) Name the heat scale once on screen. Violet for "rest" is not clear.
- **Bold push:** in cooking mode the screen _is_ the hob. The ring follows the real timer and the whole screen
  warms or cools with the step and reads from across the room.

## Overlap, and where I sit

- D1 and D2 share the ring, the dark ground, ember actions and the heat scale. Their detail screens can swap
  and nobody will notice.
- Mine shares the ring. It adds ingredient seeds and a size-by-time rack, and it is the quietest (matte, no
  glow). That is also its risk: it reads as data viz, not food.
- All three remove photos. That is bold but untested, because cooks know food by its look. One of us needs to
  keep the user's photo in round 2 and put the new idea around it, so we test both bets.

## My move for round 2

I give the ring to D2. I keep the ingredient seeds (colour by food family, size by amount) and grow them into
the main idea: the recipe as its **mise en place**, a set of bowls laid out on the screen like the counter.
While cooking, each bowl empties as its step uses it.
