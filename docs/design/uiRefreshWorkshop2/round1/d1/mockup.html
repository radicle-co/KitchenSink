The Recipe Is a Clock

Nobody cooks a document: a cook lives inside time and heat, so Commise draws every recipe as a dial whose arcs are its real steps, cool mint for prep warming to ember for heat, with one glowing point for now. Home is a time river instead of a feed, and every recipe carries a flavour fingerprint, a soft orb of light mixed from its own ingredients, so even a typed recipe with no photo is beautiful and recognisable.
