Plate Signatures

Every recipe draws its own plate: a mark generated from its real data (time as the outer ring, steps as the middle ring, ingredients as coloured seeds by food family), so a cook knows a dish by its shape before reading a word. The same mark becomes the cooking instrument on the recipe page, where the step you are on lights up on the ring and the plain text beside it stays big and straight.
