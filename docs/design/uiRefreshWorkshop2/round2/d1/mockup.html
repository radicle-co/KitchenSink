The Now Line

Every screen in Commise is drawn across one line that never moves: the now line, a band of light at the height your eyes rest, with everything earlier above it and everything ahead below it. On Home that axis is your week of cooking; on a recipe it is the method, and whatever crosses the line is lit, large and readable from the stove, while each dish is known by its own colour: its photo when you have one, a flavour orb mixed from its ingredients when you do not.
