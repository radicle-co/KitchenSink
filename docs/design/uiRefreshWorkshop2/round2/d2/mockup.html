Induction II: Live Hob. The screen is the hob, and every recipe is a burner.

Each recipe is a ring of heat (its steps, as long as they take, cool blue to red-hot), with the dish sitting inside it like a pan, and that ring is also the knob you turn to cook. The phone behaves like the appliance it lives next to: zones sized by cooking time, residual heat on what you made last night, unlit burners for what is coming, and a screen that warms with the step on the stove.
