Designer 3 · Round 2

Overhead

Commise is shot from directly above, like every cooking video: the screen is a coloured table, and each recipe is a real plate on it, its rim painted with its own time and its well holding its own food (the photo when there is one, a still life of its ingredients when there is not). Plates sit at sizes set by how long they take, ingredients wait in bowls you empty as you cook, and the step you are on rests on a chopping board while the plate's rim fills.
