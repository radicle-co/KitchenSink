Rules. Saffron (1.7:1 on fog) is light, never text and never the only sign of “now”: the pill always says it in words. In dark mode, text on saffron is always on-saffron #1A1205, never Steam (1.4:1). Ember on saffron is 3.2:1, so it is used only for large shapes. Dividers (#C3CFCA · #3A4540) are decorative; every control has a pewter edge.
