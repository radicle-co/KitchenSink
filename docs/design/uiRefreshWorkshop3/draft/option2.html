Fresh Cut

Every recipe wears a bold, flat cut-out of its own ingredients, in the bright colours of a market stall, so even a recipe with no photo looks good enough to eat. Everything around that colour is a plain, quiet app you already know how to use.

Light

Standard parts only: a large title, a carousel, plain lists, one pill button, and a five-tab bar. The colour all comes from the food. Titles never sit on art. They sit below it, on the surface.

Dark

Night is a deep aubergine, not pure black. The art keeps its colours, but each one moves a step deeper so it glows without glaring in a dark kitchen.

Palette: Greengrocer

The chrome wears quiet, cool neutrals, so the only saturated colour on screen comes from the food. Tomato is the one bright colour in the controls, and it always means “do this”. The six produce colours belong to the art alone. They never carry text or meaning.

Simple

No metaphor to learn. Lists scroll, buttons look like buttons, tabs are tabs. One Tomato button per screen.

Fresh

Bold flat cut-outs in market colours, cropped by the frame like a poster. No plates, no orbs, no paper texture.

Motion

When art first appears, its pieces land once, softly, in 0.7 s. Nothing loops. Reduced motion shows them still.
