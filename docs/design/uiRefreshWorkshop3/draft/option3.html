Workshop 3 · Option 3 · from “The Table”

Enamelware

Commise looks like your kitchen table seen from above: a calm blue enamel top, and every recipe drawn as its own dish on a white plate with a cobalt rim. Everything you touch is a plain list, a plain button and the usual tabs, so the food is the only new thing to look at.

Three screens, in light

Shown at 390 px. Each screen has one obvious primary action in cobalt, and rows are rows. The picture of the food carries the delight; nothing has to be learned.

At night, the hob

In dark mode the table becomes black hob glass and the plates turn to stoneware. The only glow is a warm pool under the dish, as if the burner beneath it were still on. Nothing else lights up.

The palette: Enamelware

Each named colour has one job. Ratios are WCAG 2.2 contrast, computed from the hex values, and every text and control pair passes AA.

The logic. The palette is one enamel plate on a blue table, and the button you press wears the plate’s cobalt rim. The food brings the warm colours, so the interface stays cool and quiet, with Yolk only for small signals like “Soon”.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for titles: warm, a little hand-cut, and narrow enough that the longest real title wraps to two lines. Figtree for reading, with tabular numbers for amounts and times.
