Fresh Cut

Every recipe wears a bold, flat cut-out picture of its own ingredients, in market-stall colours, so even a recipe with no photo looks good enough to eat. Around that colour sits a calm, ordinary app you already know how to use.

Light

Standard parts only: a large title, plain lists that scroll, one Tomato button per screen and five tabs. All the colour comes from the food. Text never sits on art.

Dark

Night is a deep aubergine, not pure black. Each produce colour steps one shade deeper, so the art glows without glaring in a dark kitchen. Tomato lightens so its label stays easy to read.

Palette: Greengrocer

The app wears quiet, cool neutrals, so the only bright colour on screen comes from the food. In the controls, Tomato is the one bright colour and always means “do this”. Danger is a cool wine, never a fill beside it. Six produce colours belong to the art alone, and none of them carries text or meaning.

Produce: art only

Each one is a ground or a shape in the art, never a UI colour. Light shade on the left, dark on the right.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for titles and facts. It is warm and a little hand-cut, like the art. Figtree for everything you read, with tabular figures for amounts. Nothing is smaller than 12 px.

Simple

Nothing to learn. Lists scroll, buttons look like buttons, tabs are tabs. One Tomato button on each screen.

Fresh

Bold, flat cut-outs in market colours, cropped by the frame like a poster. No plates, no light, no paper.

Motion

When a picture first appears, its pieces land once, softly, over 0.7 s. Nothing loops. With reduced motion, the art is still.
