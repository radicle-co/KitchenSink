Enamelware

Every recipe sits on its own glossy enamel plate, and the cobalt rim around it is the same cobalt as the one button you press. By day the table is pale enamel, by night deep graniteware, and everything you touch is a plain list, a plain button or the usual tabs.

S

Home and My recipes

390 px: Home and My recipes in light, then Home at night. One cobalt primary action per screen. Photos are cropped round inside the rim, so a snapshot taken at any angle still sits on the table. A recipe with no photo gets its own monogram plate.

Recipe detail, the whole page, by day and by night

Dark mode is the same object in its other classic colour: deep blue graniteware with stoneware plates. The rim turns a lighter cobalt and a thin gloss catches the plate’s edge. Nothing glows and nothing moves. Both pages are shown at full length so every part of the screen is visible.

No photo? A monogram plate

A recipe without a photo never shows a made-up dish. It gets a plate glazed in one of six colours, picked from the recipe’s id, with its first letter painted in the middle. Two Italian recipes look different, and every plate is honest about being a placeholder.

W B R S O M

Glazes Delft, Celadon, Paprika, Mustard, Heather and Lagoon are for plates only and never carry meaning. Letter on glaze is 5.8 to 6.7 : 1.

The palette: Enamelware

Each named colour has one job. Ratios are WCAG 2.2 contrast computed from the hex values. Every text pair passes 4.5 : 1 and every control edge passes 3 : 1, in both modes.
