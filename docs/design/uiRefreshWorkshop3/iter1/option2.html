Commise UI refresh · Workshop 3 · Option 2, iteration 1

Fresh Cut

Every recipe wears a flat picture of its own ingredients, laid out as on a chopping board with the produce cut open, so even a recipe with no photo looks good to eat. All the colour comes from the food; the app itself is black type on white, with lists and buttons you already know.

Light

A poster on white: black type, one black button per screen, and colour only where there is food. Plain rows with dividers instead of boxes. Text never sits on a picture.

Dark

Night is a soft charcoal, not pure black. The black button turns into a warm white one. Each produce colour steps one shade deeper and the pictures dim a little, so they glow without glaring in a dark kitchen.

Palette: Market White

The interface is black and white, like a printed poster. Black is the one button you press. In the controls, red only ever means delete, as everywhere else. Six produce colours belong to the pictures alone: none of them carries text, a control or a meaning. Numbers are WCAG 2 contrast ratios, light · dark; Lc is APCA.

Produce: picture grounds only

Each lead shape has one fixed ground, the colour it reads best on. Light shade left, dark right.

Type: Archivo, one family

One grotesque with a width axis. Titles are set wide and heavy, like a poster headline. Reading text is the same family at normal width. The current step grows to 22 px so you can read it from the stove.

Simple

Plain rows, a large title, tabs, one black button. Ticks, steppers and switches look like what they are.

Fresh

Produce cut open and food laid flat, in market colours, on pure white. No paper, no plates, no light.

Motion

Slices settle once, 0.6 s, when a picture first appears. A tick fills in 150 ms. Nothing loops. Reduced motion turns all of it off.
