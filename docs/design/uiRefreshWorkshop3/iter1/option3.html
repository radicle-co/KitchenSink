Enamelware

The recipe you are looking at sits on one glossy enamel plate, and its rim is a ring that fills as you cook. The rest is a calm, ordinary app on a pale enamel table, where cobalt marks only what you can press and how far you have cooked.

S O New recipe

Home and My recipes

390 px, light. One cobalt button per screen: New recipe, in its own band above the tabs, so it never covers a row. What is chosen (All, My recipes, list view) is filled in ink, so cobalt only ever means “press here”. Rows are plain list rows; their round plate is the cook’s photo or, with no photo, a glazed monogram, edged in a neutral seam.

Recipe detail, the whole page, by day and by night

The cook’s photo sits in the plate. Its rim is the cooking progress: tap a step’s number and the cobalt ring moves to that step, with the words beside it. It moves only when you tap, never on its own. The current step is larger type on a cobalt rule.

No photo? A glazed letter

A recipe with no photo never shows a made-up dish. Its round plate takes one of six glazes, picked from the recipe’s id, with the first letter in the middle. Light glazes by day, deep glazes by night. Glazes are decoration and never carry meaning.

The palette: Enamelware

Each colour has one job. Ratios are WCAG 2.2 contrast computed from the hex values; Lc is APCA 0.0.98G, for reference. Every text pair passes 4.5 : 1 and every control edge or state mark passes 3 : 1, in both modes.

Herb vs Cobalt 1.19 1.05 Herb only appears in a success message with an icon; it never fills a control.
Slate vs Ink 1.95 1.44 Muted text is also smaller (14-15 px vs 17 px). A selected chip is an ink pill with a word; a ticked box is a small square with a tick.

Type

Lexend for titles: round, open and calm, the shape of the plate in a letter. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading, built by the Braille Institute so that 1, l and I, and 0 and O, never blur. Body is 17 px; the current step is 21 px.

The two photos are drawn stand-ins for the cook’s own photos, because this page may load no outside images; every other recipe shows a glazed letter. Motion: a plate settles once on load (0.7 s, transform only), the progress ring moves 0.6 s when you change step, a tick draws in 0.15 s, the switch slides in 0.2 s. Nothing loops, and all of it stops under reduced motion.
