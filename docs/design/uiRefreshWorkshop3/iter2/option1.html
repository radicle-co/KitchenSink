The palette: Pilot Light

A cool, still room (fog by day, cast iron by night). Spruce, a deep green taken from the fog, is everything you press or have ticked. One warm light, lamplight on honey, marks what is happening now. Food brings every other colour. Status colours are words with icons, never fills.
