Commise UI refresh · Workshop 3 · Option 2, iteration 2

Fresh Cut

Every recipe wears a bold, flat picture of its own ingredients, so even a recipe with no photo looks good to eat. The app around it is black type on white, with the lists, tabs and one black button you already know.

Light

A poster on white: black type, one black button per screen, and colour only where there is food. Plain rows with dividers instead of boxes. Text never sits on a picture.

Dark

Night is soft charcoal, not pure black. The black button turns warm white. The food grounds lose a fifth of their chroma and the shapes are muted too, so nothing vibrates. Lemon stops being a fill and becomes the light: the current step gets a lemon edge, disc and label.

The shape test: 20 recipes

Twenty real-style recipes at list size (64 px). Each pairs a lead shape with a second one, and no pair repeats. A lead shape must pass the silhouette test below. Wheels, coins and rings are discs in silhouette, so they may only be second shapes.

Silhouette test: the 14 lead shapes in solid black

If a shape cannot be named in black, it cannot lead a picture.

Palette: Market White

The interface is black and white. Black is the one button you press, links are blue and underlined, and red only means delete. Six produce colours belong to the pictures alone. Every ratio below is computed in this page from the hex values: WCAG 2 ratio, light · dark, and APCA Lc for text.

Produce: picture grounds only

Each lead shape has one fixed ground. Light left, dark right. Every ground is at least ΔE00 12 from every UI colour, so no picture wears a control’s colour.

Type: Archivo, one family

One grotesque with a width axis. Titles are set wide and heavy, like a poster headline. Reading text is the same family at normal width. The current step grows to 22 px so you can read it from the hob.

Simple

A large title, plain rows, tabs, one black button. Ticks, steppers and switches look like what they are. The pictures are never buttons.

Fresh

Food cut open and laid flat, in six market colours, on pure white. No paper, no plates, no lamp.

Motion

Shapes settle once, 0.6 s, when a picture first appears. A ticked ingredient’s shape fades in 0.35 s. Nothing loops. Reduced motion turns all of it off.
