# Home, 390 px phone (Designer 0, probe v3)

**Pick: A, "Pass".** One dish waits under a warm lamp, as at a restaurant pass.

**Two brief conflicts.** "Coming soon" becomes one text line below the first screen, not rows. Recent 8 alone
is recall, so deciding leads with a short choice and reasons.

## Taste and medians

- **Type:** Recursive, one family. Its Mono axis sets facts like a kitchen ticket.
- **Food and composition:** one plated dish, top-down, inset and lit. The rest in shadow.
- **Colour:** light belongs to the food. The accent is only for pressing.
- **References:** the Hatco Glo-Ray heater over a pass (kit nobody photographs). Jan Groover's 1978–80
  kitchen still lifes (rare on moodboards).
- **Refusal:** a full-bleed hero cut by the edge.

**Category median:** white cards, a Recent carousel, a serif, green, Coming-soon banners. **House answer:**
near-white, a big grotesque name, an edge-cropped hero, a strict list, grey plus one dark accent. My first instinct
matched it.

## Frame

**Job:** "What do I cook now?" **Success:** one tap to cooking. **Pretty only:** people admire it, then open
Recipes. **Primary:** "Back to step 4" with a timer strip when cooking. "Start cooking" when deciding. "Write your
first recipe" when empty.

**Deciding facts:** total time and days since last opened. Cook history and pantry data do not exist yet. This is
a gap.

**List ledger:** Recent and the other picks are vertical, because you scan and compare them. Nothing is
horizontal.

**Concepts:** a dish under the pass lamp (A), and choosing tonight like a ride picker (B). Struck as medians or
canon: a poster name, a dish wash, a receipt. Struck as encoding or horizontal: hob rings, day dots, hourly light, a
ticket rail.

## A, "Pass"

1. **Top bar:** the wordmark, and **New recipe** as an outlined 44 px pill with a +. Not the job, so top right.
2. **Signature:** a fixed elliptical pool of Lamp light, 22% to 0 over 220 px. It falls on the ground above and
   around an inset photo, 358×240, radius 12. The photo is never tinted.
3. **The pick**, on flat ground past the falloff. The name is 28/32 Bold, up to 3 lines. Below it, in 16 px Mono
   Muted: "35 min · Serves 4 · Not opened in 3 weeks".
4. **Or:** two 64 px rows, each with a reason and a chevron.
5. **Recent:** its heading shows at y ≈ 620, above the primary, as a reason to scroll. Then 8 rows of 72 px with
   72×54 photos, **All recipes · 142 ›**, and "Coming soon: meal plans, shopping list, nutrition."
6. **Primary:** Lemon, 358×56, fixed above a standard opaque tab bar. Only the page scrolls, vertically.

**Argument.** Thesis: one plate lit in a dark room. Trajectory: iOS 26 Liquid Glass and Music's lit art treat UI as
lit material. The step past them is one fixed, opaque light with no glass. Closest app: Apple TV, separated by the
light pool. Not in 2022, because dark UI was flat #121212. The signature encodes nothing and has no text, so it
survives the longest title at 320 px and 200%. It is banned from cook mode, lists, controls and behind text.

**Palette "Pass"** (cast iron, lemon, lamp). Deep in both themes. "Light" is raised for daylight. Cook mode
stays neutral. The content hue is Lamp `#FFB46B`, used only in the pool. Light/dark: Range ground
`#2A2F34`/`#111316`, Steel surface `#353B41`/`#1A1D21`. Well sunken `#22272B`/`#0A0B0D`, Rim edge
`#929AA2`/`#7E868E`. Chalk ink `#F8F9F7`/`#F0F1EE`, Smoke muted `#BAC0C5`/`#B2B8BD`. Lemon action
`#F2DA3A`/`#EAD13C`, Seed on-action `#1E1A00`/`#1C1800`. Pith wash `#423D1C`/`#2A2712`, Rind warning
`#FFB46E`/`#FFA85C`, Chilli danger `#FFB0A8`/`#FFA49B`.

## B, "Three pans"

A chooser, not one pick. White "pan" cards on steel. The signature is "Tonight",
set once in Big Shoulders Stencil, after the stencilled marks on Cambro food pans. Three equal radio cards, 132 px
each, stack vertically, with the first preselected. Each has **Recipe ›**. The primary is "Start cooking · Pad see
ew". At 200% text, photos stack over text.

**Argument.** Thesis: three plates on a steel pass. Trajectory: Material 3 Expressive puts display type over plain
controls. The step past it is a cut stencil. Closest app: Uber's ride picker, separated by the stencil on steel. The
signature is one word, so it survives 320 px and 200%. It is banned from cook mode, lists and controls.

**Palette "Hotel pan"** (steel, beetroot). Light/dark: Steel ground `#E2E5E7`/`#16191B`, Plate surface
`#FFFFFF`/`#202427`. Pan sunken `#D3D7DA`/`#0E1012`, Rim edge `#6E767C`/`#80888E`. Char ink `#15181A`/`#F4F6F7`,
Grease-pencil muted `#4A5157`/`#B5BCC1`. Beet action `#9C1D55`/`#FFA6CC`, On-beet `#FFFFFF`/`#2A0716`. Stain wash
`#F5DCE6`/`#3A1C2A`, Mustard warning `#7A4E00`/`#F5B85A`, Tomato danger `#A8250F`/`#FFA894`.
