# Critique: Proposal A, "The Now Line"

**Verdict: do not ship.** Users must learn the fixed line to read any screen. Heuristic judgement, untested.

## 1. Render

Rendered with Playwright at 390 and 320 px, and at a rough 200% text. All five phones seen.

## 2. The set

Siblings: `final/proposalB.html`, `final/brief.md`, and `round1/` and `round2/` `d1`, `d2`, `d3`. A and B are
**distinct**: a vertical stream against a free overhead layout, and fog `#EEF3F1` against enamel blue `#C9D6EC`.

## 3. What must survive

No owner praise is on record. The energy: heavy Funnel Display titles, the warm saffron stroke, and the big
cook-mode timer with labelled Back and Next step.

## 4. What fails

- **The signature encodes meaning.** A title shrinks to 60% and fades as it leaves the line, so size and colour
  show "distance from now". At 200% the "Sunday" pill covers "Cookies".
- **"Now" is false.** On Home it marks the roast, whose own line says "Yesterday".
- **Home helps nobody decide.** It has no filled primary, and its list of recent recipes is recall. Three "Soon"
  rows fill the lower half of the first screen.
- **A control hides on scroll.** "Next step" is hidden on arrival. A step must reach the line before it appears,
  and then it covers step 4's text.
- **Idle motion.** The gradient moves over 16 s, and the dot pulses every 5 s.
- **One colour, many meanings.** Action `#B3330E` also colours every quantity, which you cannot press. It is ΔE00
  4.4 from the line's tomato and 5.6 from a standard danger red.
- **Cook mode.** The step text is 21 px, below 26 pt. Three steps show at once. A saffron wash turns the lower
  screen beige.
- **The photo is not the hero.** It is a 200 px circle, cropped off the top right.
- **At 200%, the tab labels run together:** "DiscoverRecipesCollections".

## 5. The direction

Keep the type and the warm stroke. Drop the line as structure. Use a familiar large-title stack with a real
rectangular photo on top. Home asks "How long have you got tonight?" (20, 40 or 60+ min). It then shows three
recipes, each with a reason from its stored time and servings. **Start cooking** is the filled primary.

The signature is a static **saffron stroke**, a 6 px bar set into the baseline of each large title. It encodes
nothing, never moves and never enters cook mode. Cook mode shows one step at 28 pt on a neutral, opaque ground.

**Palette "Aubergine & Saffron"** suggests a market stall, with deep skins against bright spice. Action `#6A2C5A` light (9.1:1) and `#E6A6D6` dark (9.6:1).
The content hue is saffron, `#E2961C` light and `#FFC04D` dark, for decoration only. The action colours are ΔE00 29
or more from every earlier action colour, and 19 or more from danger red.

## 6. Not run

The full role table, APCA, the prior-art search for the stroke, a mockup of the direction, real OS text scaling,
and a wet-hands test.
