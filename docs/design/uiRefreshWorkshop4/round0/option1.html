Carton

Every recipe is packed like good groceries: its name printed big on a sky-blue carton, the food seen through a window cut in it. Around that one carton is the plainest app you know, so the food and the one button are the only loud things on the screen.

Option 1 · Designer 1 · palette “Carton”: Sky carton, Tomato action, white shelf. Illustrations stand in for the cook’s own photos.

Light

One carton per screen, holding the thing the screen is for. Everything else is a white shelf with plain rows, tabs and one Tomato button.

Dark

Dark follows the phone’s setting. The carton deepens to night-sky blue; Tomato lightens so it still reads as the one thing to press.

Cooking, first run and other states

Cook mode has no carton, no glass and no photo: one step, big, with Next and Previous fixed at the bottom. The other states use the same parts with real copy.

Palette “Carton”

A white shelf, one sky-blue carton and one Tomato action. Blue sits opposite the browns, golds and reds of cooked food, so the dish in the window looks warmer. Tomato is the only colour you can press. Contrast below is computed in this page from the same tokens the phones use.

Colour distance (CIEDE2000, script output): Tomato is 45–68 from every earlier-round teal and at least 15.1 from last round’s Paprika, Copper, Beetroot and Aubergine. Inside the palette, Tomato, Sky carton, Mustard and Radicchio are at least 19.1 apart in both themes.

Light

WCAG ratio and APCA Lc on the Shelf. Text on the carton is Ink only; the facts band is Shelf on Ink.

Dark

The same roles at night. Ground L ≤ 0.25; Ink to Muted ≥ 1.6:1.

Type

One family, Bricolage Grotesque, used two ways: condensed and heavy for names printed on the carton, open and plain for reading.

Principles applied

  • Fresh in the look, familiar in the structureTabs, large titles, rows with chevrons, a stepper, chips and a docked button are exactly where people expect them (Jakob). The carton is the only new thing, and it is a picture, not a control (canon §2.1, §2.2 rules 1–3).
  • Text never on the photo, colour never on the foodThe name is printed on the carton above the window, so no scrim is needed and the photo is never tinted (§3.4 rule 4).
  • One loud colour means pressTomato fills one button per screen and marks ticks, links and the current tab. Sky blue only ever holds pictures and type (§3.4 rule 1, von Restorff).
  • Vertical to read, sideways only to sampleIngredients, steps, nutrition and the library run down. Filter chips are the one sideways row, with the next chip peeking (§1.3).
  • Home helps you choose with facts we holdQuickest dinner, your own 5-star rating, feeds 6: total time, ratings and servings are stored today. No invented history (§4, data audit).
  • Two modes, two layoutsPlanning gets colour and the carton. Cook mode is opaque and neutral, 28 px, with fixed Next and Previous low on the screen (§4).
  • CalmNothing moves at rest. Presses scale to 98% in 100 ms; a tick fills in 180 ms; all of it is off with Reduce Motion (§4.1).
