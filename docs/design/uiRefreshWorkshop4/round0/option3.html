Commise visual refresh · Workshop 4 · Option 3 · Designer 3

Food Edge

Every list in Commise leads with a column of food that runs down the edge of the screen, so you choose dinner by looking, then confirm by reading.

The rest is the cold, clean light of an open fridge: icy grounds and deep blue-black ink, so warm food is the warmest thing you see, and Persimmon marks the one thing to press.

Light: Home, Recipe detail, My recipes

390 px phones. Plain tabs, large titles, plain rows, a stepper, checkboxes and one filled Persimmon button per screen. The recipe page is shown at its full length, scrolled.

Home in its other two states

Home shows the cook's state. Cook marks live in memory on the phone and in the tab's session on the web, so "Cooking now" shows only while that session holds them. With no recipes yet, Home asks for the first one.

Dark: Home and Recipe detail

Dark follows the phone's setting. The food stays the brightest thing, and Persimmon turns to a lighter tone so it still reads as the one action.

Palette: Cold Light

The idea: the light of an open fridge door at six in the evening, cool and clean, so roast skin, salmon and tomato look warm and good to eat. Colour lives in four places: the icy ground, the blue-black ink, the Glacier content hue behind food, and Persimmon on the one thing to press. Every figure below is computed on this page from the hex values (WCAG 2.x ratio, APCA 0.0.98G Lc).

Type: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, one family

Drawn by the Braille Institute for readers with low vision: open shapes, a 1 that is never an l, a 0 that is never an O. A cook reads through steam at arm's length; this face was made for hard reading, and at 800 it is also warm and confident.

Principles applied

The food edge is look, not a control

It is the row's picture, made bigger and set flush to the screen edge. The whole row is one target with a chevron. Ignore the edge and nothing is lost: the title, time and reason are all in words (canon 2.2 rules 1 to 3).

Vertical for lists you go through

Picks, recent recipes, the library, ingredients and steps scroll down. Only the filter chips scroll sideways, as optional peers with the next chip peeking (1.3).

Home helps you choose

Three picks with reasons built only from stored facts: total time ("Ready by 6:45 pm" is total time plus the clock), your own star rating, a collection you made, difficulty and servings. No invented history (4, data audit).

One primary, in thumb reach

Start cooking on Home and on the recipe; New recipe on My recipes. Each is the only filled Persimmon button on its screen, low and labelled (Hick, Fitts, von Restorff).

Quantities sit inside the steps

Steps carry their amounts in bold and their times as timer buttons. The servings stepper rescales both lists. The current step is marked with a wash, a filled number and the words "You are here" (Cowan, 4).

Chrome serves the food

No tint on photos, no text on photos, no glass. Cool grounds flatter warm food; Persimmon never sits next to Raspberry in the same role, and Danger is a text row, never a pill (3.4).
