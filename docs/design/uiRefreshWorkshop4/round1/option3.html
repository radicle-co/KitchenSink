Commise visual refresh · Workshop 4 · Round 1 · Option 3

Food Edge

In Commise the food sits against the edge of the screen, like the plate in a field guide, and the words face it on a clean strip. You choose dinner by looking, then confirm by reading.

The palette is Stainless & Yolk: the cool grey of a kitchen sink, so the food is the warmest thing you see, and the yellow of an egg yolk on the one thing to press.

Light

390 px phones. Three tabs, large titles, plain rows, one yolk button per screen. The recipe page is shown at full length, scrolled.

Dark

Dark follows the OS setting. Home is shown in its other state: a cook in progress, read from the cooking session the device holds. The recipe page is the same page as above, clipped to the first screen.

Palette: Stainless & Yolk

The cool, brushed grey of a home kitchen sink and the yellow of an egg yolk. A grey ground makes every dish the warmest thing on screen; yolk is the colour of food at its most appetising, and here it marks only what you press. Principles applied

One signature, in the look only

The flush food plate is each recipe's own photo, placed at the screen edge. It encodes nothing: title, time and reason are all in words. It is banned from cook mode, controls, sheets and the ingredient and step lists (canon §2.2 rules 1–3).

Grouping by region, then space

Each plate and its caption share one white strip; strips sit 16 px apart on the grey, twice the largest gap inside a caption (§1.2 proximity, common region).

Scroll direction

Picks, ingredients, steps and the library are vertical. The only row is the filter chips, with the next chip peeking (§1.3).

A short choice with stored reasons

Three picks, each with a different stored fact: your stars, your collection, the cooks' rating, and a ready time from total time plus the clock (§4).

One primary, low and labelled

One yolk button per screen, in the lower half. Links are Saffron and underlined; Delete is a text row, apart (§1.1 Fitts, von Restorff; §3.4 rule 3).

Large text is real

Each phone is a container. At 200% text the plate stacks above its caption and stays at the edge; nothing is hidden (§2.2 rule 11, WCAG 1.4.4).

Heuristic judgement, untested with users. Food images are drawn stand-ins rendered for this mockup, not photographs.
