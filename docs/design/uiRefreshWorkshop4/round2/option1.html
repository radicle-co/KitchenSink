Commise visual refresh · Option 1 · Designer 1 · round 2

Carton

Each recipe is packed like good groceries: its name printed big on a sky-blue carton, the food seen through a die-cut window, the facts on a dark band at its foot. Around that one carton sits the plainest app you know, so the food and one tomato-red button are the only loud things on the screen.

Light

One carton per screen, holding the thing that screen is about. Everything else is a standard tab bar, nav bar, list, stepper and button.

Dark

Dark follows the phone’s setting. The carton deepens to night blue and the band goes near-black, so it no longer outshines the food. The food holds the brightest pixels on the screen.

Cooking and other states

Cook mode has no carton and no photo: it is neutral, opaque and big. The Home primary lands on its first screen.

Palette “Carton”

A white shelf, one sky-blue carton with a nori-black band, and one tomato. Blue sits opposite the browns, golds and reds of cooked food, so the dish in the window reads warmer. Tomato is the only colour you can press. Contrast below is computed in this page from the same tokens the phones use.

Light

WCAG ratio and APCA Lc on the Shelf. Text on the carton is Ink; text on the band is Shelf.

Dark

The same roles at night. The band stays near-black in both themes.

Type

One family, Bricolage Grotesque, in two voices: printed (heavy, display optical size) for the name on a carton, plain for everything else.

Principles applied

  • Fresh in the look, familiar in the structureThree tabs, a nav bar, rows with chevrons, a stepper, chips and a docked button sit where people expect them (Jakob). The carton is the only new thing, and it is a picture, not a control (canon §2.1, §2.2 rules 1–3).
  • Food first, text never on the photoThe name is printed on colour above the window: no scrim, no tint. At large text the window moves to the top, so the dish stays on the first screen (§3.4 rule 4, §1.4).
  • One loud line, one filled buttonThe title drops to a nav-bar size so the carton’s name is the only shout. One Tomato button per screen, and it does something the carton does not: the carton opens the recipe, the button starts cooking (von Restorff, information scent).
  • A short choice, not a heroThree dinners show above the tab bar at 390 px, each with a reason the recipe holds (§4, Hick).
  • Vertical to read, sideways only to sampleIngredients, steps, nutrition and the library run down. Filter chips are the one sideways row, with the next chip peeking (§1.3).
  • Two modes, two layoutsPlanning gets colour and the carton. Cook mode is opaque and neutral, 28 px, with fixed Previous and Next low on the screen (§4).
  • CalmNothing moves at rest. Presses scale to 98% in 100 ms; a tick fills in 180 ms; all of it is off with Reduce Motion (§4.1).
