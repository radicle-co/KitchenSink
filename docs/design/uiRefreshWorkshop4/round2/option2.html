Deli Counter

Your dinners stand in tiers, like trays in a deli case: real home cooking, cropped into the pan, on a pistachio shelf with its name tag hanging below. Everything you press is the plain tab bar, list, stepper and one prosciutto-red button you already know.

Light

390 px phones, 6:10 pm on a Tuesday. One filled button per screen. The shelf is the one expressive element: it appears under food on Home and the recipe page, and never in a list row, a control or cook mode.

Dark

Light and dark follow the phone's setting. The button keeps its cured red at night, a little lighter so it holds 3:1 against every dark surface, with a white label.

The other states of Home, and cook mode

Home keeps its title, "Tonight", in every state. The content and the one button change. Cook mode is neutral, opaque and plain.

Palette: Prosciutto & Pistachio

An Italian deli case: cured ham behind pale green glass, on pistachio shelves. The ground is the glass, faintly green. The one thing you press is the deep red of prosciutto: near-black in light, a lighter cured red at night. Pistachio is the shelf edge and the mark on an empty tray; it never carries text or a control. The red is darker than any dish in light, so it reads as the button, not as food. Principles applied

Familiar structure, fresh look

Tab bar, large title, back, stepper, checkboxes, chips, one filled button. What is new is only how food stands: a tray on a full-width shelf, its tag hanging below. The shelf encodes nothing and has nothing to tap (canon §2.1, §2.2 rules 1–3).

Food wins

Home cooking, cropped into the pan, at 16:9 and full tray width. Nothing covers the food: the tag hangs below the shelf. No tint, no text on a raw photo (§3.1, §3.4 rule 4).

Scroll direction

Dinners, ingredients, steps and the library are vertical, because you read or compare them. The only row is the filter chips, optional, with the next chip peeking (§1.3).

Home helps you choose

Three dinners for three stored facts: total time, ingredient count, servings. "Ready by" is the stored total time added to the clock, set no bigger than the name. No invented reasons (§4).

One action, owned

One filled Prosciutto button per screen, low, with a word on it. On Home it sits 8 px under its dinner and 28 px above the next, and says the dish: "Cook the green curry" (Hick, Fitts, von Restorff, proximity).

Large text keeps the food

At 200% text the tray keeps at least 120 px, the tag grows below it, and step numbers move above their text so lines stay long enough to read (§4.1).

Cooking needs nothing remembered

Quantities in the steps, durations as timers, the current step marked. Cook mode: one 28 px step, labelled Next and Previous, screen on from your tap. Motion: a press and a tick only, off under Reduce Motion (§4).
