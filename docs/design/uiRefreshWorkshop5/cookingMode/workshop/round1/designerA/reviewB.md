# Review of designer B: the shape of the whole cook

Reviewer: designer A. I read the rationale and all 13 renders (phone, tablet 1194, web 1280).

## What to steal

- "Before you start" (phone board 10). It shows the whole job, the free time and the first alarm before the cook commits. This is the best answer to CK-007 in round 1.
- "Nothing needs you for 38 min." It names the wait as free time. That is calm, and it serves CK-027 and CK-036.
- The timer offer at the start of a wait ("Start · 38 min"). The cook does not have to set a timer by hand (CK-020).
- "Coming up · after the oven beep". It names what starts the next step, so look-ahead has a trigger (CK-033).
- The in-app alarm as a sand card above the next line, with +5 min. It is loud without taking the whole screen (CK-030, CK-031).

## What fails

- The phone strip is a chart in a 64 pt band. Its labels are 13 pt, which meets the type ramp, but a two-lane chart is unreadable from three feet (CK-016). The owner's bar is calm and not dense, and a Gantt chart on every phone screen reads as project management (CK-027).
- The tablet and web screens have four zones: ingredients, step, timers and the whole-cook line. Each zone is good. Together they are the densest screen in the round (CK-021, panic when too much moves).
- "Then, in the order you like" and "Do it now" depend on order data that recipes do not carry. Without it the feature shows nothing, or shows a guess. An app-chosen order scored 3.5/10 in Hamada (CK-003, CK-022).
- One action line in focus at 34 pt, with the rest at 17 pt after "Then:". The step text in the render still runs four lines at the large size, so the focus is a paragraph, not a line (CK-034).
- The 834 tablet is not drawn (P5).

## One question

If the to-scale line moves off the step screen and lives only in "Before you start" and one tap away, what does the cook lose while cooking?
