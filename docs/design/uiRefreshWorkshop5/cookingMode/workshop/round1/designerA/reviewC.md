# Review of designer C: glance from across the kitchen

Reviewer: designer A. I read the rationale and all 11 renders (phone, tablet, web 1280).

## What to steal

- The far band. The action, its amount and the time left sit at 40 pt, and that size meets the 3 ft reading goal (CK-016). My 30 pt reading size is weaker, and I will test 40 against it.
- The band follows the state: doing, up next, waiting, two timers. One place on the screen always answers "what now" (CK-008, CK-020).
- The finished-timer screen. One inverse surface, a 112 pt Stop, and +5 and +10 min. Nobody misses it with wet hands (CK-002, CK-030).
- "Alarms are off. If the phone locks or is on silent, this timer will not ring. Allow alarms." This is honest copy at the right moment (CK-030, CK-036).
- "While it roasts · if you like". The tasks are optional and come from later steps (CK-033).

## What fails

- The far line repeats the checklist. "Mash 3 tbsp butter with the lemon zest" shows at 40 pt and again as the second tick row. Every line says the same thing twice, and that is density, not calm (CK-027).
- A cook who never ticks sees a stale far line. The designer names this risk, but no screen draws a fix (CK-008, CK-034).
- 40 pt fits about 15 characters per line. The real step text wraps to three lines and pushes the list down (CK-034). Every step has to be rewritten as short action lines, and imported recipes do not come that way (CK-006).
- Strikethrough is everywhere. On tablet, nine of eleven ingredients are struck through in one column, and the screen reads as noise.
- The tablet ingredient rows for this step use a steel-tint band. That is steel as a fill, against P4. The "Step 2" text label already carries the meaning.
- The inverse alarm screen is right for a timer, but it is the one moment in the round that is not calm. Keep it for alarms only.
- The 834 tablet is not drawn (P5).

## One question

If the far band shows only the time left and the step count, and the action stays in the list at 17 pt, does the glance still work?
