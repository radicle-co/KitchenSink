# Designer B reviews designer A, round 1: "Now and next" (heuristic judgement, untested with cooks)

## Steal

- **"Next 2 steps go fast", with chips for what to have out.** The best single idea of round 1, and the only
  warning before the time-critical stretch in any direction (CK-021, CK-022). Mine has nothing like it.
- **A Done button that names the move:** "Next: trim the beans". The cook knows what the tap does (CK-033).
- **One cue inside a wait:** "At 1:10:00, turn the oven down. You will be told." One fact and a promise (CK-036).
- **The tablet "Done" record.** After an interruption it shows the place in one glance (CK-008, CK-020).

## What fails

- **Every action line is 30 pt, so nothing is in focus.** Three big lines with three ticks is a wall of type.
  CK-034 asks for each action in order: make the next line big and the rest 17.
- **The phone is dense.** A Now card, a Next card with three chips, a dock and the nav make four blocks on a 390
  screen. That misses the owner's calm bar (Mela, Things 3). On a phone, Next can be one line.
- **The wait shows 1:12:40 twice,** in the card and in the dock. R5: show each fact once.
- **The in-app alarm is easy to miss from across the room** (CK-030). It is a dock row at the bottom with a normal
  Stop, and the Now card only gets a sand edge.
- **R1 is broken.** The sheet uses a 2/4/8/12 segmented control. R1 asks for a servings stepper at the top.
- **The order is still the app's.** "Done, next step" leads one way. Only the waits offer a choice (CK-003).
- **30 pt on a phone falls short of SC-007 at 3 ft** by A's own figures. The trade is honest, but it is a gap on
  the device most cooks have.

## Question

A cook taps "Do it now" on "Trim the beans" (step 5) during step 4. What do "Step 4 of 8" and Done say next?
