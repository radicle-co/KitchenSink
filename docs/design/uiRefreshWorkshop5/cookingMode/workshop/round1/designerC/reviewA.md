# Review of designer A ("Now and next"), by designer C

## Steal

- **"Next 2 steps go fast".** The only direction that answers research problem 5 (CK-021, CK-022). It lists what to
  have out before Done. The fast stretch is where food burns, and my direction has nothing for it.
- **"After that, nothing needs you for about an hour."** One plain line turns a wait into permission to rest (CK-036).
- **A Done button that names the move**: "Done, carve and serve". It confirms the place without a second look (CK-008).
- **Ingredients in the order of use**: For this step, Coming up, Used (CK-001, CK-020). Better than my tagged list.
- **The tablet's Done column**: a record that finds the place again after an interruption (CK-020).

## What fails

- **Too many boxes.** The phone stacks a Now card, a Next card, a sand card, chips and a dock, each in a frame. The
  brief says "separate groups with space, not boxes", and Mela and Things 3 do not frame every block. It reads as a
  dashboard, not calm.
- **30 pt fails SC-007 on a phone.** A's own figure is 12.8′ at 3 ft. "Tablets are read from afar" covers the stand
  (CK-032), not the cook whose only device is a phone, and that is most cooks.
- **The in-app alarm is a dock row.** A cook at a pan across the kitchen does not see a sand row or an outlined card.
  In the app, all of it rests on sound (CK-030).
- **The wait screen shows 1:12:40 twice**, in the top card and in the dock: two big elements, one fact.
- **Goal titles need data nobody has.** "Butter the chicken" is not in a typical recipe. A parser that invents it is
  a new failure point, and an author who must write it has a new chore (CK-034).
- **"Do it now" on a step 5 task.** Moving the step pointer loses step 4 (CK-008). Not moving it makes the button do
  nothing visible.

## Question

The cook taps "Do it now" on "Trim the beans · step 5" during the roast. Where are they afterwards? How do they get
back to step 4 and its timer in one tap?
