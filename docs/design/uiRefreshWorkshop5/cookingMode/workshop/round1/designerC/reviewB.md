# Review of designer B ("The shape of the whole cook"), by designer C

## Steal

- **The timer offered at the moment it helps**: "Set a timer for the potatoes? Start · 38 min". It is the one pattern
  here with a positive study behind it (Jaber 2024, CK-036).
- **"Nothing needs you for 38 min."** The best wait headline of the three: a fact, not a countdown.
- **"Coming up · Step 4 · after the oven beep."** It names the event that starts a step, not only its number (CK-033).
- **The alarm permission in Before you start,** with what No means (CK-030). Better than my notice mid-cook. Keep both.
- **"The sound repeats until you stop it."** One line that builds trust.

## What fails

- **The You and Food strip is on every phone screen.** It is 64 pt of tiny marks and 13 pt labels. It is unreadable
  from a metre, and up close it is chrome to decode. The shape of the job is P3's job on Before you start (CK-007).
  On every step, a chart becomes the frame of the screen. Mela and Things 3 do not do this.
- **Clock times are a promise the oven breaks.** "Out at 5:57" and "at the table about 6:15" assume the recipe's
  durations, and the first spec says to check doneness, not the clock. If the roast runs long, the Now marker drifts
  and the cook seems to fall behind. That is the panic in CK-021.
- **The core idea rests on data that does not exist.** "In the order you like" needs dependencies between steps, and
  B admits no recipe carries them. Wrong data makes a "ready now" step not ready: a wrong dish, not a slow one (CK-003).
- **Four sizes and a title**: 34, 17, 15 and 13, plus 28. That breaks the three-size rule (brief §3). The step at 34
  wraps to five lines.
- **The in-app alarm is a tinted band at the top of the step.** It is better than a dock row. But only a cook who
  already looks at the screen sees it (CK-030).

## Question

The roast runs 20 minutes over. What do the strip, "out at 5:57" and "at the table 6:15" show? The food is not done,
and the cook is not late. How does the time line keep from saying they are?
