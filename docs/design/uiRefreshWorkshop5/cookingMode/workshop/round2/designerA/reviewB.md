# Review of designer B, round 2: the shape of the whole cook

Reviewer: designer A. I read the rationale and all 15 renders. Heuristic judgement, untested with cooks.

## What to steal

- The shape shown once, on Before you start, and then told in words. The strip left the step screens, and the words
  carry it well: "Nothing needs you for 38 min", "Nothing needs you until the potatoes timer rings" (CK-033, CK-036).
- No clock times while cooking: "10 min past its 1 h 30. The oven sets the pace" (CK-013). A and C still show them.
- Wait jobs that are only getting ready: fetching and measuring. They are safe in any order and need no "Do it now"
  control. This answers the question both reviewers asked about my round 1 job from step 5 (CK-003, CK-022).

## What still fails

- 34 pt fails SC-007 by B's own figure: 14.5′ at 3 ft, under the 16′ minimum. The rationale names the gap and keeps it.
  For the cook whose only device is a phone, that is the one requirement this screen exists to meet (CK-016).
- Every unticked line is 34 pt, so a four-line step is a wall of type with no focus. On the step 2 board the last line
  is cut under Next ("Put the lemon and 1 head garlic" ends mid-word). That is the round 1 fault again (CK-034, CK-008).
- Goal titles stay: "BUTTER THE CHICKEN", "FILL THE TIN", and the button "Done · next: fill the tin". Recipes do not
  carry them. Both reviewers raised this in round 1, and the rationale does not answer it (CK-006).
- The Next row truncates an instruction: "Fill the tin: 800 g potatoes, 3 carrots, ...". An amount cut by an ellipsis
  is a wrong amount on a quick glance (CK-001).
- The current step in All steps uses selected-fill, a steel tint on a whole row. P4 allows steel on a control, not on
  a surface.

## One question

If 34 pt stays, which cook does B expect to read the step from 3 feet, and on which device?

If round 3 converges on one design, its base should be C, because B misses SC-007 at 34 pt and still needs goal titles.
