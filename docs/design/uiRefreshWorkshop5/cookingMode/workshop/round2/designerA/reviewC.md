# Review of designer C, round 2: glance from across the kitchen

Reviewer: designer A, who authored A. I read the rationale and all 16 renders. Heuristic judgement, untested.

## What to steal

- The preview is the button: "Next · Step 3" over "Toss the vegetables in oil". The next step shows once. This fixes
  the duplication in my own round 2, where "Then" and the button both point ahead (CK-033).
- "You ticked 2 lines · Show". The record says what the cook did, not what the app assumes. That is the most honest
  answer to Fix 3 in the round (CK-008, CK-020).
- "Time the turn? Start · 35 min". A timer offered for a wait the recipe does not time (CK-031).
- "Nothing needs you for 8:12", with the countdown inside the sentence, and jobs that are only getting ready (CK-036).

## What still fails

- Every unticked line is 38 pt, so step 2 does not fit: four lines need a "More below · scroll" cue, and the fourth line
  is cut mid-word. A cook who never ticks sees the same wall all through the step. That is honest, but nothing marks
  the place (CK-034, CK-008).
- "Up next" shows the whole next step at 38 pt in a muted grey. Large muted text reads as disabled. Contrast is not
  checked here (NFR-004, CK-016).
- Clock times stay: "The roast is out at 11:31". The oven breaks that promise (CK-013). B dropped them.
- The button's second line is 13 pt on a filled button. It is the look-ahead, and it is the smallest text on screen
  (CK-033, CK-016).

## One question

If C keeps every line at 38, what tells a cook who never ticks which line they are on after an interruption?

If round 3 converges on one design, its base should be C, because its model is the most honest to the data and meets
SC-007. It needs A's single focus line or B's words for the shape to cure the wall of type.
