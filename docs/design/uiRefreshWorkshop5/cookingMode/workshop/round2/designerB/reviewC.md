# Designer B reviews designer C, round 2 (heuristic judgement, untested with cooks)

## Steal

- **Ticked lines fold into "You ticked 2 lines · Show".** The record is true and costs one row. My ticked lines stay
  in place at 17, which is worse (CK-020, Fix 3).
- **The next step lives only in the button:** "Next · Step 3 / Toss the vegetables in oil". It names the move, it
  shows the next step once, and nothing on screen goes stale (Keep 2, Fix 2, CK-033).
- **A timer at the line that names the time,** and "Up next" filling the body once all lines are ticked (CK-031).
- **The wait speaks plainly:** "Nothing needs you for 35 min. Then turn the potatoes." And "Have these out before
  the beep" is the best wording of the fast warning in the round (CK-021, CK-022).

## What still fails

- **Clock times.** The wait says the roast is "out at 11:31". The juices cue beside it is right. But the clock time
  is a promise the oven breaks. At 11:35 with a pink roast, the cook reads that they are late (CK-021).
- **The headline swaps units:** "for 35 min" in one state, "for 8:12" and "for 12:30" in others. A countdown in the
  headline also makes it a second timer beside the dock. Pick one form (Fix 2).
- **Before you start is thin.** It says "about 2 h 05, hands busy for 25 min", but it does not show where the free
  time falls. A's timed lines answer CK-007, and C's do not.
- **A long step hides the next step.** Step 2 at 38 needs a scroll ("More below"). Until the cook ticks every line,
  the next step is only the 13 pt subtitle of a button, which is not readable from a metre (CK-033).
- **The fast warning needs an author flag.** Without it, nothing shows. That is honest, but most recipes will lack
  the flag, so the Keep point is mostly absent in practice. Say how the flag gets written (CK-021).

## Question

The roast runs 20 min long. At 11:35, what do the wait headline and "out at 11:31" say?

If round 3 converges on one design, its base should be C, because nothing on its screen can go stale. Carry in A's timed Before you start and "Do it now", and drop the clock times.
