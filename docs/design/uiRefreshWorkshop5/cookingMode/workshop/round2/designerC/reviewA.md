# Review of designer A, round 2 ("Now and next"), by designer C

## Steal

- **The calm.** One line at 38, the rest at 17, with space and no frames. These are the screens of the three that
  stand closest to Mela and Things 3. Mine fill the phone with 38 and must scroll.
- **"Do it now" keeps the place.** The job becomes the Now, the roast goes to the dock, and the button reads "Done ·
  back to step 4". It answers my round 1 question fully (CK-008, CK-033).
- **A two-part timer offered in one tap:** "Start a 1 h 30 timer. It rings at 20 min to turn the oven down, then when
  the roast is done." One offer covers the step. "The phone will ring" promises no more than it can (CK-030, CK-031).

## What still fails

- **The focus line goes stale again.** "Now · 2 of 3" at 38 is a claim. Only a tap on Done moves it. A cook who
  does lines 2 and 3 without a tap still sees line 2 as Now, in the biggest type. The 17 pt text is true, but the
  glance is wrong (Fix 3, CK-008). It is my round 1 fault, moved to a button. One tap a line taxes the expert (§3b).
- **A clock time is a promise.** "Roast · out at 5:57" on the wait screen is the same flaw I raised against B in
  round 1. A roast that runs long makes the cook look late (CK-021).
- **Keep 2 is weakened.** "Done · step 5 of 8" names a place, not a move. The next step's words in the button, not
  under "Then", meet both Keep 2 and Fix 2.
- **The fast warning is a sand-filled block** with four items in a sentence. It is the one heavy shape on a calm
  screen. An icon and a word carry it without the fill (Fix 1).

## Question

A cook does all three lines of step 2 and never taps Done until the end. During that time, what does the 38 pt line
say, and why is it not false?

If round 3 converges on one design, its base should be A, because its screens are the calmest and closest to the
owner's bar. Its one blocking fault, the focus claim, can be fixed without changing the layout.
