# Round 2 brief

Round 1 produced three directions and six reviews. The reviews agree on the points below. Round 2 keeps each direction
its own, but each one takes these points in. Read every review of your own work before you start.

## Keep, in every direction

1. A warning, one step early, that the next steps go fast (A).
2. A Done button that names the next move, for example "Next: trim the beans" (A).
3. "Nothing needs you for 38 min" during a wait, with at most two optional jobs from later steps (B).
4. A timer offered at the moment the step needs it, not only on the step that names a time (B).
5. Alarm permission asked once in Before you start. If the cook said no, an honest "Alarms are off" notice (B, C).
6. A finished timer that reaches a cook across the room: a full-screen alarm in the app, and the system alarm on a
   locked phone (C).
7. Ingredients grouped by the time of use: now, later, already in (A, B).

## Fix, in every direction

1. Density. Fewer cards and frames. A screen holds one clear focus, and the rest is quiet. Judge it against Airbnb,
   Mela and Things 3.
2. Nothing on screen twice. The current action, a timer or an amount shows in one place.
3. Nothing stale. If the cook never ticks a line, the screen still tells the truth.
4. No step order the data cannot support. If a direction needs to know which steps can run in parallel, it says so as a
   data requirement and also works without it.
5. Steel is an accent, never a fill (P4). R1's servings stepper stays a stepper.
6. The reading size. Pick one and justify it from research.md for a phone that is the cook's only device. The round 1
   figures were 30, 34 and 40 pt.
