## Defects

- Major (CK-033, CK-001). The Next button is now the only look-ahead, and on the phone it cuts its line. "Lift the
  chicken onto a board…" loses "for 15 min", and "Toss 800 g potatoes, 3 carrots…" loses the onions and the oil. The
  rationale says the cut falls only between ingredients, but the first one cuts an instruction. Fix: make Back an

- Minor (CK-002). "Do it now" is a 44 px pill with 13 pt text. Make the whole job row the target.
