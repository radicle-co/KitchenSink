# Round 3 brief: one design

Round 2 votes: A and B named C as the base, and C named A. Round 3 converges on one design. Designer C builds it on C's
round 2, with the grafts below. Designers A and B then review it, and designer C makes the final fixes.

## The base: C

- Nothing on screen goes stale. Lines the cook ticks fold into one record ("You ticked 2 lines"). Next ticks nothing.
- The next step shows once, in the Next button.
- A finished timer takes the whole screen in the app, with the system alarm on a locked phone. "Alarms are off" is
  honest about a cook who said no.

## Grafts

1. From A, the single focus. The first unticked line is the one line at the cooking size (38). The other unticked lines
   sit under it at 17, under "Then". This ends C's wall of 38 pt text on a long step. A cook who never ticks still sees
   every line, so nothing goes stale.
2. From A, "Do it now" on a get-ready job keeps the cook's place and offers "Done · back to step 4".
3. From A, the timed lines on Before you start, and ingredients grouped as Now, Later and In already, with the R1
   stepper.
4. From B, the All steps view, with each timer under the step that started it, opened from the step name.
5. From B, the plain words for the shape of the cook: "Nothing needs you for 35 min", "Step 5 goes fast". When the
   food runs long, the screen says "checking", never "late".
6. From B, the get-ready jobs during a wait: fetch and measure what a later step uses. They need no new recipe data.

## Drop

1. Clock times such as "out at 11:31". The oven does not keep them. Show time left only.
2. A headline that switches between minutes and a countdown. Use one form.
3. Step titles such as "Butter the chicken". Imported recipes do not have them.

## Deliver

Write to `round3/designerC/` (mockup.html and rationale.md). Draw every core screen from the main brief, plus Before
you start, All steps, the get-ready job in progress, Alarms are off, and done. Draw them on phone 390 in light and dark,
tablet 834 and 1194, and web 1280 and 768. The rationale lists the recipe data each feature needs and what it does
without that data.
