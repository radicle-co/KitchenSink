Lemon and thyme roast chicken

Steps

Edit

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Take the chicken out of the fridge, pat the skin dry with paper towels, and leave it at room temperature while the oven heats.

  2. 2
    You’re here

    Mash the 3 tbsp butter with the zest of 1 lemon, half of the thyme leaves (from 3 sprigs) and the 1 tsp salt. Work your fingers under the skin of the breast and spread the butter over the meat.

  3. 3

    Scatter the 800 g potatoes, 3 carrots and 2 onions in a large roasting tin and toss with the 2 tbsp olive oil. Put the chicken on top with the halved lemon, the 1 head garlic and the rest of the thyme in the cavity.

  4. 4

    Roast for 20 minutes, then lower the oven to 190°C (170°C fan) and roast for another 1 hour 10 minutes, until the juices run clear.

    1 h 30 min
  5. 5

    Rest the chicken, loosely covered with foil, for 15 minutes before carving. Spoon the pan juices over the vegetables.

    15 min
Servings
4

Chicken

  • 1 whole chicken about 1.8 kg
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter softened
  • 1 lemon zested and halved
  • 6 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 head garlic halved crosswise
  • 1 tsp flaky sea salt

Vegetables

  • 800 g waxy potatoes quartered
  • 3 carrots cut into chunks
  • 2 red onions cut into wedges
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Freshly ground black pepper to taste
