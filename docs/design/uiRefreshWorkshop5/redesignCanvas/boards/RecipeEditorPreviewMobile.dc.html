Brown Butter Lemon Chicken

40 minTotal
15 minPrep
25 minCook
EasyDifficulty

A fast skillet dinner: crisp-skinned chicken thighs in a lemony brown butter sauce with capers. Ready in about 40 minutes and good over rice or orzo.

More

Ingredients

for 4
8 Chicken thighsbone-in, skin-on
0.25 cup All-purpose flour
2 tbsp Olive oil
3 tbsp Unsalted butter
3 cloves Garlicminced
0.5 cup Chicken broth
1 Lemonzested and juiced
2 tbsp Capersdrained
2 tbsp Fresh parsleychopped

Steps

1

Pat the chicken dry and season all over with salt and pepper. Dredge lightly in flour and shake off the excess.

2

Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken skin-side down until deep golden, then flip and cook until browned on the second side.

12 min
3

Move the chicken to a plate and pour off most of the fat. Add the butter and garlic and cook until the butter foams and turns nutty brown.

3 min
4

Stir in the broth, lemon juice and capers, scraping up the browned bits. Return the chicken, cover and simmer until cooked through.

10 min
5

Finish with the lemon zest and parsley. Taste and adjust the salt before serving.
