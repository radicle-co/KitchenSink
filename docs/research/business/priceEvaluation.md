## Pro at $99.99 or $59.99: feature comparison and projection

> **Note (2026-10-29).** This is a dated research file, copied as it was written. Owner rulings made after it
> supersede its recommendations. The decisions in force are in
> [`docs/business-plan.md`](../../business-plan.md#current-decisions-2026-10-29) and the
> [010 plan register](../../../specs/010-subscriptions/spec.md#plan-register).
>
> Written on 2026-10-27, before the later rulings that day. Those kept Pro at $99.99 and gave Pro a pantry
> with use-by dates, AI meal plans on its 30 uses, and household sharing. The table in section 1 shows the
> earlier position. The revenue projection in the original (sections 3.1 and 3.2) is left out, because it comes
> from the private financial model.

At $59.99 a year, Pro needs 1.36 to 1.75 times more paying cooks to earn what $99.99 earns. No price test we found shows a price cut lifting conversion by more than about 1.23 times. The revenue evidence therefore favours $99.99, and the competitive evidence is the risk.

Samsung Food+ sells more AI for $59.99, and on documented rows Pro adds little above it. So $99.99 works only if Pro leads with what Samsung does not document: capture from anywhere, migration and export, offline use and checked data. This paper shows the evidence so the owner can decide, and it says how to make $99.99 work.

Scope: the launch offering, every spec except 015 (publishing rewards) and 018 (Chef Program), per the owner's ruling [R]. AI use: one call to the model is one use [R]. A weekly plan made in one call is one use.

**Reading.** Every single-app test found a price change of 30% to 50% moved conversion by 5% to 19%. The lift $59.99 needs, 1.36 to 1.75 times, is several times larger than any response observed. No test covers a 67% change on a yearly recipe plan, so this is a GAP, not a proof.

### 4. What the evidence supports, and what would flip it

#### 4.1 The reading

**On revenue, the evidence supports $99.99.** At the same conversion, $99.99 earns more per payer than $59.99 (an internal model, not published). To close the gap, the price cut must lift paying cooks by at least 1.36 times. No cited test shows a response that large.

**On competition, the evidence warns against $99.99.** Samsung Food+ costs $59.99 and adds AI meal plans, use-by planning, a pantry and grocery ordering. On documented rows, Pro adds only trainer plans. $99.99 is also above RevenueCat's top yearly price figure ($90) [P].

The two readings meet in one question. **Do cooks see Pro as Samsung Food+ for more money, or as something Samsung does not offer?** The answer decides conversion at $99.99, and today it is a GAP.

#### 4.3 The fastest way to confirm the price

The owner ruled out a slow A/B test as the way to set the price [R]. Two faster signals are available in the first weeks of launch:

1. **Trial starts per paywall view.** This reads within days, not months. RevenueCat's median download-to-trial for high-priced apps is 8.9% [P]. Plan target: at or above 8.9% of downloads start a trial at $99.99.
2. **Cohort 1 day-35 paid share.** This reads at day 35. Plan target: at or above the median band point of 2.1% ([RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps/), freemium median).

#### 4.4 What would flip the reading to $59.99

| Signal                                                 | Threshold                                                  | Why it flips                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trial starts at $99.99                                 | Below 4.4% of downloads, RevenueCat's low-price median [P] | Cooks are treating Pro as overpriced at the first paywall      |
| Cohort 1 and 2 day-35 paid share                       | Below 0.51%, the ReciMe first-year point [D]               | At that level no price change on its own closes the gap        |
| A credible estimate that a cut lifts conversion        | 1.36 times or more                                         | $59.99 then earns as much or more                              |
| Samsung Food documents bulk import, export and offline | Any of the three                                           | Pro's remaining edge over Samsung shrinks to trainer plans     |
| Household stays a paid add-on                          | Owner rules for a seat price                               | Pro is then behind every $59.99-class plan on a validated need |

Conditions that would strengthen $99.99: cohort 1 converts at or above the median, migrations are common in week 1, and trainers appear among the payers.

### 5. Gaps and open items

- **Price elasticity for recipe apps.** GAP. No test covers a 67% yearly change in this category.
- **Samsung Food export, bulk import and offline.** GAP. They decide how much Pro adds above $59.99.
- **Household plan.** Open owner decision (017-FR-034 against 010).
- **Video capture.** Pro's deepest edge is specified, not built. Beta cannot show it.
