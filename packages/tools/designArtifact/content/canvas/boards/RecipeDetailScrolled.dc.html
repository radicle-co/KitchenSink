Ingredients

for 4
Edit

Chicken

  • 1 whole chicken about 1.8 kg
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter softened
  • 1 lemon zested and halved
  • 6 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 head garlic halved crosswise
  • 1 tsp flaky sea salt

Vegetables

  • 800 g waxy potatoes quartered
  • 3 carrots cut into chunks
  • 2 red onions cut into wedges
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Freshly ground black pepper to taste

Steps

Edit
  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Take the chicken out of the fridge, pat the skin dry with paper towels, and leave it at room temperature while the oven heats.

  2. 2

    Mash the butter with the lemon zest, half of the thyme leaves and the salt. Work your fingers under the skin of the breast and spread the butter over the meat.

  3. 3

    Scatter the potatoes, carrots and onions in a large roasting tin and toss with the olive oil. Put the chicken on top with the halved lemon, the garlic and the rest of the thyme in the cavity.

  4. 4

    Roast for 20 minutes, then lower the oven to 190°C (170°C fan) and roast for another 1 hour 10 minutes, until the juices run clear.

    1 h 30 min
  5. 5

    Rest the chicken, loosely covered with foil, for 15 minutes before carving. Spoon the pan juices over the vegetables.

    15 min
