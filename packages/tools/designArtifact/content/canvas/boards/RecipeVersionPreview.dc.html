Title
Lemon and thyme roast chicken
Description
A bright, herby roast with a crisp skin.
Servings
4
Prep time
15 min
Cook time
75 min
Total time
90 min

Ingredients at v3

  • 1.8 kg Whole chicken
  • 2 Lemon
  • 6 sprig Fresh thyme
  • 50 g Butter360 cal
  • 4 clove Garlic

Steps at v3

  1. 1.Heat the oven to 200°C.
  2. 2.Rub the chicken with butter, lemon zest and thyme.
  3. 3.Roast for 75 minutes, until the juices run clear.
