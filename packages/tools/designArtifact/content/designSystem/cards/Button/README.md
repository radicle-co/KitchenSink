The labelled action control. One filled `primary` per view.

Tiers: `primary` (action fill, white label), `secondary` (paper, `line-control` edge, ink label), `ghost` (`action-text` label, no surface), `destructive` (danger label; filled only in the confirm tone of a dialog). Sizes: `lg` 52, `md` 44, `sm` 36 visual px, all with a 44 px hit area. The glyph is required for all tiers but `ghost`. The label is the accessible name. A busy button shows a spinner and stays focusable on web.
