ChromeSurface is the translucent bar material behind top and bottom bars. It blurs content under it by 12 px and goes solid where the reader asked for reduced transparency or more contrast. The edge prop names the side that meets content and carries the hairline: top for a bottom bar, bottom for a top bar. It paints behind its parent with -z-10, so the parent must be isolate and positioned (relative, fixed or sticky).
On native it is real Liquid Glass on iOS 26 and a solid raised surface with a hairline elsewhere, and it takes no touches; `visible` can hide the material until content scrolls under the bar.
