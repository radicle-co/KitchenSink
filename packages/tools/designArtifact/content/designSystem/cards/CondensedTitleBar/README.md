The 56 px bar a pushed screen shows once its large title scrolls out of view. It keeps the back control one tap away and repeats the title in one line, with an optional ⋯ `menu` at the end. `visible` is display derivation from the screen's scroll host, not a mode. The title is hidden from assistive tech because the H1 already says it. Mount it on pushed screens only; a top-level page shows none. On native the bar sits on the floating layer (Liquid Glass on iOS 26, a solid surface elsewhere) and hides by that surface's own visibility and the title's opacity, never by an opacity on the bar.
