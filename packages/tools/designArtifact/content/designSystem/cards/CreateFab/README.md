CreateFab is the floating "New recipe" or "New collection" button on phones and tablets. It draws a solid action-colour pill with a glyph and a label, and shrinks to a 56 px disc while the page scrolls down, growing back on scroll up, at the top, or on focus. It also stays a disc when its label would take more than half the window, hides while the keyboard is open and in the first-run state, and is hidden from the 840 px width up where the sidebar holds the action. The label is always the accessible name, even in the icon-only form. Place it right after the page's H1 in DOM order so screen-reader users meet it early. On native it floats over the screen's foot as a sibling after the scroller inside the screen's ScrollHost, 16 pt in on phones and 24 on tablets, and takes the end-side safe-area inset when the phone is held sideways.
