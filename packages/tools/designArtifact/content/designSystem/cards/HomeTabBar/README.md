HomeTabBar is the bottom tab bar of the app below 840 pixels on web, and on every phone and tablet on native. It draws the reachable destinations (Home, Recipes and Discover today) as a 24-pixel glyph over a label, on the bar material from ChromeSurface. The active tab is told by a 3-pixel bar above the glyph, a heavier label and `aria-current` on web or the selected state on native, never by colour alone. The web leaf takes `chrome`, `locale`, `liveCapabilities` and `activeId`; a second tap on the active tab goes to the tab's root, then the top. The native leaf takes `onPress` and `onLongPress` in place of a route, plus the safe-area insets and a `compact` flag that puts the label beside the glyph when the window is under 480 tall. Place it once per shell and pass it localized `chrome` copy.
