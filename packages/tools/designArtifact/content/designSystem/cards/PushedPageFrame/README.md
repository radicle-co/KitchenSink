PushedPageFrame is the one frame every pushed page mounts: collection detail, version history, data sources and profile. It owns the page's one `ScrollHost` and pins a `CondensedTitleBar` over the scroller. So Back is in the bar at every scroll position, and the page has exactly one Back. The props are `back`, `headingId` (the page's H1), an optional one-line `title` and `menu`, and a `children` render function. The `label` of `back` is the accessible "Back to {parent}". On native the page's scroller spreads `bind` and merges `inset` into its content style, so the content starts under the 56 pt bar. On web the document scrolls, `bind` is unused and `inset` is empty. The bar shows its surface once the page scrolls, and its title once the large title has gone under it. Web at 840 and wider has no bar, and the large title's eyebrow link is Back there. The spec is `docs/design/uiRefreshWorkshop5/pinnedBackBar.md`.
