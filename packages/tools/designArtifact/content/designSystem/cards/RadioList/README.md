RadioList lets a person pick one choice from a short list, drawn as a column of whole-row radios, such as the bulk import's choice of how to match ingredients. Each row is its own bordered card at least 48 px tall, with its 18 px radio first, its label wrapping rather than scrolling at 320 px, and an optional `hint` under the label. Use it only for a real radio group, where moving through the options may change the choice. A list of foods is not one, because the arrow keys would pick a food, so foods use `ChoiceRow`. The list is controlled and may hold no choice: with `value` undefined nothing is checked, and the list never checks a row by itself. Every string is localised by the caller.
