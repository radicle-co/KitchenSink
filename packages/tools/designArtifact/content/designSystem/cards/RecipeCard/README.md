RecipeCard shows one recipe as a link in one of three variants: `grid` (the full card with a 4:3 cover and five rows), `row` (an 80 px cover with the title and two meta lines for lists) and `compact` (cover and title, for Home on narrow containers). It takes a `recipe` model, a required `headingLevel` of 2 or 3 on web, `onSelect` and, on web, an `href` that makes it a real link; native ignores `href`. The whole card is one control named by the title. Absent difficulty, cuisine and tags render nothing, an unrated recipe says so, and a draft shows its status in place of its visibility. The `nutrition` slot takes a node, and an absent slot draws no item. Both platforms ship a leaf with the same variants.
