RecipeHero is the opening of a recipe's detail page: its photos as a swipeable carousel, or a 96-pixel monogram band when the recipe has none. The carousel is the page's only photo surface, because the first photo is the cover. Pass `recipeId`, `title`, optional `cuisine` and `photos` in display order; an empty `photos` array gives the band, tinted by the recipe id. The optional `overlay` holds the back and more-actions controls, drawn over the top edge and first in reading order, and the caller owns what they do. The hero holds no state and fetches nothing, and the band is decorative because the page title names the recipe. It has a web leaf and a native leaf with the same props and the same two states.
