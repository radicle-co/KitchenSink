SectionSwitch is a sticky 48 px bar of links to a page's sections, with the current one marked and room for one trailing control (the recipe page puts "Screen on" there). It is a `nav` of real `#id` links so the page works without script; a press jumps through the screen's ScrollHost, which scrolls the heading under the sticky chrome, focuses it and records the hash. The current link takes `aria-current="location"`, drawn as ink at weight 600 over a 3 px bar. The current section comes from the caller's scroll spy via `currentId`, and the links scroll inside the bar while the trailing control stays pinned. It needs a ScrollHost above it, and all labels are caller-supplied localized copy.

On native the links are at least 44 pt tall and a press jumps through the screen's ScrollHost.
