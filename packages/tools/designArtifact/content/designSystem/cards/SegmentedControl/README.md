SegmentedControl shows two or three views of one thing in a single look with two semantics, chosen by `form`. `route` segments are places (My recipes, Collections): on web they are links in a `nav` with the current one `aria-current="page"`, driven by `current` and `onSelect`, and `width` can be `share`, `content` or `adaptive`. `view` segments are presentations of the same content (list, grid): a `radiogroup` driven by `value` and `onChange`, where only view segments may carry an `icon`, and `labelVisibility="hidden"` draws glyphs alone while keeping each label as its name. The union makes the two unmixable, and every label is the segment's visible text and accessible name.
The native leaf draws both forms as pressable segments with no links, and the route form calls `onSelect`.
