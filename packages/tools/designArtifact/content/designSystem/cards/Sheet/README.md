Sheet is the controlled modal dialog for focused tasks, built on Radix Dialog: full screen below the `sm` breakpoint and a centred dialog from `sm` up. The `size` prop is `content` (as tall as its content), `full` (always the available height) or `page` (page-sized, up to the 72 rem detail column). It takes a required `title` (the dialog's heading), an icon-only close control named by `closeLabel`, an optional `toolbar` pinned under the title, a scrolling body and a `footer` of actions pinned under a hairline. Every close route (Close, Escape, overlay, swipe) calls `onOpenChange(false)`, focus moves to the title on open and returns to the opener on close, and `onDismissed` fires once after it is off screen. With a toolbar, keep text inputs in the toolbar controls and not in the body, since the sheet collapses beside an on-screen keyboard. On native it is a bottom-anchored Modal over a dimmed scrim that clears the system bars by their insets; Android back and a swipe down also call `onOpenChange(false)`, and `size` `page` is the same as `full`.
