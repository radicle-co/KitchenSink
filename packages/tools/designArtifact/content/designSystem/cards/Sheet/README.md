Sheet is the controlled modal dialog for focused tasks, built on Radix Dialog: full screen below the `sm` breakpoint and a centred dialog from `sm` up. The `size` prop is `content` (as tall as its content), `full` (always the available height) or `page` (page-sized, up to the 72 rem detail column). It takes a required `title` (the dialog's heading), an icon-only close control named by `closeLabel`, an optional `toolbar` pinned under the title, a scrolling body and a `footer` of actions pinned under a hairline. Every close route (Close, Escape, overlay, swipe) calls `onOpenChange(false)`, focus moves to the title on open and returns to the opener on close, and `onDismissed` fires once after it is off screen. `screenReaderLanding` is `title` (the default) or `content`. Use `content` only for a sheet opened to show one specific thing, such as the Needs review notice after a jump. Then the content's own focus move is the only one. The option changes only where the screen reader lands, on web and native alike. With a toolbar, keep text inputs in the toolbar controls and not in the body, since the sheet collapses beside an on-screen keyboard. On native it is a bottom-anchored Modal over a dimmed scrim that clears the system bars by their insets; Android back and a swipe down also call `onOpenChange(false)`, and `size` `page` is the same as `full`.

When to use: a focused task on a phone, as a bottom sheet. From 640 px the web Sheet is still a centred modal, and a centred modal is only for a step that must block everything (owner ruling 2026-11-17, docs/design/modalUsage.md). A form, picker, list or detail on a wide screen is drawn as a side sheet. Use `presentation="dialog"` only for a short decision before a commit.
