A dashed chip that stands where a missing value would be, such as "Private ingredient" in place of a nameless recipe line. Tone `neutral` means nothing the reader can fix here; `caution` means the reader can act on it. The dashed outline marks the words as a stand-in, but the words carry the meaning, so outline and tint are never the only signal. It is plain text with no role or aria-label, and it wraps at spaces rather than truncating. The caller decides when a value is absent and supplies the localized words. On native the chip is a View holding a Text, because nested Text takes no border or padding, and the words stay readable to the screen reader.
