StateNotice is what a view says when it cannot show its content and the cook must choose what to do next: a failed read, a missing recipe, a crashed screen. It takes a title, an optional body and at most two actions (a primary and a secondary, each with a label, a glyph and onPress); the title is announced assertively. On web it sits in the page's flow and an action with an href is a real link; on native it stacks the actions in the bottom third of the screen, primary on top, and ignores href. Pass localized strings, since the design system holds no copy.
