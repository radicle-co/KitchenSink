# Issue Ledger — Feature 003 (source-agnostic food data) — Review 2 synthesis

**Role:** synthesis adjudicator (adversarial). **Phase:** pre-implementation design-baseline.
**Goal of this loop:** confirm the docs (and the load-bearing migration artifacts the build will start
from) are correct/complete enough to START implementation.
**Inputs adjudicated:** the six `review2/*` lens findings + `decision-register.md` (16 D-\*) +
`.stabilization/inputs/{staff-review,autoresolutions}.md`. Every high-stakes claim was re-verified
against the actual source files (`plan.md §2`, `v-model/module-design.md`, the hand-authored
`packages/services/food-service/src/db/**`, `v-model/trace.md`, `v-model/hazard-analysis.md`).

**Verdict:** ISSUES — **12 blocking** confirmed defects (4 DB-artifact, 6 design-pseudocode, 2 test-gate).
The canonical _design docs_ (plan §2, ARCH-006, MOD-006) are largely correct on the hardest integrity
questions; the blockers are (a) the hand-authored schema/test artifacts regressing the model, and
(b) lifecycle/queue/budget control-loop contradictions inside the MOD-\* pseudocode.

## How verification resolved a reviewer conflict

`design-correctness.md` listed "Refresh vs manual pick" as **PASSED** (trusting the MOD-020 narrative at
`module-design.md:1995-1997` that "only the changed fields are re-pulled"). Reading the **actual drain
path** (`module-design.md:466-531`, `processRow`) shows MOD-004 has **no refresh branch** — it always
full-fans-out by `food.name` and re-merges. The impl-readiness H2 finding is therefore correct and the
design-correctness PASS is **overturned** (see DSN-4, and R3 in the rejected table). Reconcilers must not
rely on that PASS.
